Learnvora, logo in use, 18 Oct 2026

One logo, three handles.

The mark is a disc with a V bitten out of it: a v biting an o, the "vo" of vora. The master is gold. Each handle swaps the disc and the v of the wordmark to its accent and changes nothing else, so three accounts read as one family on a phone.

The family

One master, three colourways

The avatars are the real 1080 px files shown as Instagram shows them, in a circle. Real display sizes below, with dsa.learnvora as the example.

Master lockups, horizontal and stacked: gold disc, gold v, ivory letters on navy; all navy on a light ground. A handle uses the same lockup with its accent on both the disc and the v: gold for ai.learnvora, violet for dsa.learnvora, cyan for systemdesign.learnvora.

Days 1 to 6 of each series, cropped the way the Instagram grid shows them (the centre 3:4 of each 9:16 cover), newest first. The avatar carries the brand here, which is why the covers keep the mark below the crop.

Clear space and minimum size

Room to breathe, and how small is too small

Clear space x is half the diameter of the disc, on every side. Nothing enters it: no text, no edge of the frame, no other logo. On an avatar the disc is 59 percent of the circle, so the clear space is far larger than the minimum.

VersionSmallest on screenSmallest in print
Mark alone16 px (below 32 px use the small-size cut, favicon.svg)5 mm
