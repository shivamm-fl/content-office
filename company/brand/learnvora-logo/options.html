Learnvora, logo exploration, 18 Oct 2026

Six ideas, one logo.

Accepted by the owner: the Bite, with one change. The v in the wordmark is filled with the same colour as the disc, and the master (company) logo is gold. Everything below shows the accepted version; the exploration that led to it is unchanged.

Learnvora is learn plus vora, voracious: learn voraciously. The brand needs one master mark that works as a round avatar, as a favicon, in one colour, and that three handles can wear in three accents without becoming three brands. Below: the placeholder judged, six different concepts sketched on one grid and put through the same tests, the one I chose, and why.

The result, master: gold disc and gold v, ivory letters. Tests used for every concept: 16 px, 48 px, the 110 px circle crop Instagram shows, one colour only, navy on a light ground, and next to the real wordmark.

Step 0

The placeholder, judged

the placeholder at 168, 16 (zoom x4), 48 and as the 110 px circle

Verdict: replace it. Keep the navy, the accent and the round terminals.

  • It is a tick. At 16 px the two legs lose their colours and it reads as a check mark.
  • Nothing owns it. A plain v says nothing about learning or about devouring, and thousands of v marks exist.
  • The join is a drawing glitch. In the 1080 px avatar the accent leg lands on the ivory one and leaves a stray ivory sliver at the join.
  • The bar has no job. It floats under the v, pushes the pair up and makes the tile bottom-heavy in the circle crop.
  • It needs two tones. The ivory leg vanishes on a light ground, and in one colour it is only a v and a bar.

Steps 1 and 2

Six concepts, one grid, the same tests

Every sketch is drawn on a 120 by 120 grid in the brand colours. Gold stands in for the accent. The 16 px tile is a real 16 by 16 raster blown up four times with hard pixels, so you see what a phone shows.

01

The Bite

Chosen

A V bitten out of a disc. Negative space is the letter, the bite is the verb.

16 px zoomed x4, 48 px110 px circle crop

Passes every test. At 16 px it is still a disc with a notch. In one colour nothing is lost, because the idea is the shape, not a second tone. The disc is already a circle, so Instagram's avatar crop never touches it. The V is negative space: you read the letter instead of seeing it drawn. Risk: a wedge cut from a disc nods to Pac-Man. An upright V, no eye, and the narrow letter angle of the wordmark's v keep it ours.

score 13 of 14

02

Open Pages

Rejected

An open book whose two pages are the legs of a v.

16 px zoomed x4, 48 px110 px circle crop

Clear story, weakest ownership. An open book is the most common education symbol there is, and the V is only implied by the page tops, so at 16 px it reads as an M or a flag. The wide flat shape also runs into the edge of the circle crop, and in one colour only a thin spine gap separates the pages, which closes at 16 px.

score 9 of 14

03

l + v

Rejected

One stroke: the l stem becomes the left leg, the right leg rises from its foot.

16 px zoomed x4, 48 px110 px circle crop

It is a tick mark. A tall stem with a diagonal off its foot says done or verified, not learn. The l and the v never separate at any size, so it reads as neither letter, and a tick is the least ownable shape there is.

Steps

Rejected

A stepped chevron, one block per day, the last block is today.

16 px zoomed x4, 48 px110 px circle crop

Good idea, wrong drawing. One block per day links to the progress bar, but nine small blocks collapse into dust at 16 px, look like bricks in the circle crop, and give a pixel look that fights the calm clean style. It also repeats the progress bar already on every cover.

Jaws

Rejected

A v as jaws, a morsel held in the mouth.

16 px zoomed x4, 48 px110 px circle crop

It is a person. A ball held in a V reads as someone with raised arms, or an ice-cream cone. Charming, but it invites a mascot reading and the brand decided on no mascot. The accent dot is also the first thing lost at 16 px.

Ribbon

Rejected

A bookmark whose swallow-tail notch is the v.

Step 3

Chosen: the Bite

Construction on the 120 grid. Gold is the mark, dashed lines are the guides.

  1. The story is in the shape. Vora means voracious: something took a bite. And the mark is the v and the o of "vora" in one picture: a v biting an o.
  2. It is a circle. Instagram shows avatars as circles, so the mark and the crop are the same shape and no corner is ever cut. The disc also gives each handle one big flat area of its colour.
  3. One shape, no inner detail. It survives 16 px, one colour, a light ground and a small embroidered patch, because nothing inside it needs a second tone.
  4. The bite is the wordmark's v. The notch angle comes from the v counter of Space Grotesk 700 (16 degrees), opened to 22 so it still reads at small sizes (27 in the favicon cut). Corners are rounded to match the pill and the progress bar on the covers.
  5. Flat and calm. No gradient, no outline, no glow, which matches the clean style of every reel and cover.
  6. The v wears the disc's colour. The owner's change. The bite and the letter it came from now share one colour, so the eye runs from the mark into the name, and the other letters stay ivory so the accent remains one small deliberate hit.

The v in the wordmark has a narrow counter. The mark's bite takes that same upright V and opens it a little for small sizes.

How it was refined, and what was dropped

Keep, refine or replace? Replaced. What survives from the placeholder is the navy ground, one accent per handle and round terminals. The shape, the construction and the story are new.

Step 3 continued

Colourways

The rule: the disc and the v are always the same colour, and nothing else is. A handle uses its accent for both: gold for AI, violet for DSA, cyan for System Design. The master (company) version, which the website uses when no track is active, is gold #F2B544, the same gold as ai.learnvora. The other letters are ivory on dark. The bite is never filled: it is the background showing through. On light grounds use the all-navy version, because the pale accents fail contrast on white. The all-ivory version exists only for one-colour jobs such as stamps and embroidery.

Step 4

Wordmark: lowercase, Space Grotesk 700, outlined, the v coloured

The disc is a heavy shape, so a light weight looks thin beside it. 700 also matches every cover headline. The letters are converted to outlines with fontTools: the variable font is pinned to the weight with varLib.instancer, kerning pairs (n and v, v and o, r and a) come from the font's own table, tracking is minus 1 percent. No live text is left in any SVG. The v is cut out as its own path so it can take the disc colour while the other letters stay ivory.

The disc runs from the baseline to the top of the l with one unit of overshoot on each side. The gap between disc and l is a third of the disc. In every SVG the v path has id="v" and the disc path has id="disc", so a web component can recolour them from CSS.
