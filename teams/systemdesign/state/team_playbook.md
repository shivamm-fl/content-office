## Motion designer
- 2026-10-19 (s001): new_reel.py gives scene #sA only; add one `<div class="beat" id="sX">` per scene before `check`. `M.W` keys are the caption token without punctuation (`fresh`, not `fresh,`), use the nth argument for repeats. Fade a whole card by collecting its children in an array, or text under the next card overlaps. A 112 s day with 8 scenes held at one new object every 3 to 4 s.
- 2026-10-19 (s002): phone and server rack drawn as small SVG-div helpers (same shape, same place every day); a stamp or label placed over a menu panel overlaps its rows, so size the panel for the last row first. A per-word `M.W` key is case-sensitive (`Server` vs `server`).
- 2026-10-20 (s003): `M.W` keys drop punctuation on both sides (`kaam:` is `kaam`, `jawab.` is `jawab`); a key with a comma throws. A repeated word needs its index (`at("F","page",0)`), a missing index silently falls back to the first. Anything that stays on screen across a step (kick label, TIME strip) must be faded when the next step in the same scene starts, or `check` reports content_overlap. A TIME strip of five segments (one red segment fills per step) carried the "each step costs time" idea across scenes C to F.
- 2026-10-22 (s004 DNS): `M.W` keys drop every `.` so `.com` is `com`. A vertical chain of boxes (same x/y in each scene, earlier nodes dimmed) carried a five-step lookup across scenes C to E; pointer answers are small green chips beside the box plus an up arrow. A shrinking bar (scaleX to 0, ease none) is a cheap TTL clock. Register new icons with `Viz.ICONS.name = (c) => ...` before `M.box`. Shell `npx hyperframes` has no network here: use the office `hyperframes` tool for check and snapshot.
- 2026-10-23 (s005 HTTP/HTTPS): vertical layout (phone on top, server at the bottom, wire between, the message card in the middle) reads bigger on a phone than left/right. The kit's arrow layer sits under opaque cards but shows through a card that recolors with a translucent fill: recolor the border only. A text swap inside one card must hide the old text with a 0.01 s tween first, or `check` flags content_overlap. `M.W` is case-sensitive (`Server` at a sentence start); `check` names the missing word. Labels placed over the dashed wire collide: use a stamp (opaque box) instead.
- 2026-10-24 (s006 TCP vs UDP): a sequence diagram (two boxes, two dashed lifelines, diagonal arrows for handshake, squares flying across for packets) carried TCP and UDP in different scenes with the same frame; the arrow handle has `hide(t)` but no `.el` (use it, not `.el`, or tl.to gets undefined and `check` reports `_gsap`). Let the sender's own squares fly (no ghost copies) or `check` reports content_overlap on every number. A lost packet should slide sideways while the others pass, not stop in the lane.
- 2026-10-24 (s006 FIX): the visual reviewer fails a scene whose first frame is only a heading: put the scene's frame objects (boxes, chips, an icon) on screen at scene start, not on their spoken word. A closing scene needs pictures too (mini file squares and a waveform with a gap beside the TCP and UDP stamps), not two text rows. `M.W(scene, word, n)`: a word spoken twice in a scene needs the right index (`nahi` at 53.8 vs 57.5) or the object appears early.
- 2026-10-25 (s007 REST vs gRPC): one fixed frame (ORDERS on top, PAYMENTS below, dashed wire) in every scene, with only the thing on the wire changing (text card vs dots block), made a 10-scene day easy to follow. Moving cards must stop short of the boxes (a card that travels 190 px up overlaps the top box). A label over a dashed wire needs its own opaque background (`lab(...).style.background`). A cross drawn over an icon hides it: put it beside or on the object that cannot be read. A multi-step scene (chain, then zones) must hide the first half before the second half appears. The 4-word label "LIKE A LOCAL FUNCTION" collided with the block; one to three words only.
- 2026-10-25 (s007 FIX): `M.fit` scales by the widest child, so a scene label spanning 1080 px (a full-width kick) blocks all scaling and leaves the picture small with an empty band below: keep kicks about 700 px wide. Show the empty shells of later panels at scene start so frame 1 is not a lone heading. Any label that appears for one beat needs its own `gone()` or it sits on the next beat's bars.
- 2026-10-26 (s008 latency vs throughput): one canteen counter (COUNTER box with a cup icon, same shape every scene) plus a clock card for latency and a cup tally for throughput carried a 10-scene concept day; a count-up (`{u:0}` tween with onUpdate, "+0 to +20 MIN") made the growing wait visible, and a drawn SVG curve (stroke-dash reveal) showed latency climbing at the limit. An `EXAMPLE` pill placed within 60 px of a box overlaps it after `M.fit`: leave 90 px between a pill and the next object. Hook text is in `M.hook`; the queue (8 person icons) sat under it with the first two already on screen at frame 0.
- 2026-10-28 (s010 availability nines): `M.W` keys drop every `.` and `,`, so `99.9%` is `999%`, `0.1` is `01`, `8,760` is `8760`; a number spoken twice needs the index. A ladder of cards with red downtime bars on ONE shared scale (999 to 540 px, 99.9 to 54 px, ...) showed "each nine = 1/10" without words; draw the empty dashed slot first, fill it later. Cue phrase indices count `||` groups, not `|` pauses: check before `make_voice` (an out-of-range cue crashes it). M.box with h 130 and a 70 px icon crowds the label: h 160 and icon 56.
- 2026-10-30 (s011 SPOF): a fixed vertical chain (USER, LOAD BALANCER, SERVER, DATABASE) at the same left position in A, C, D, F, L with stamps in a right column (x 660-1020) made 13 scenes read as one story; twin layouts (two boxes of 420 px, dashed "+" slot that fills) for the redundancy scenes. `M.W` keeps `%` (`99%`, `9999%`) but drops `.` and `,`. A label that sits where a moving stamp passes needs its own `gone()` (the L "+ BACKUP" label). Cue phrase index counts `||` groups.
- 2026-10-31 (s012 recap): a recap of five stops used one tracker strip (DNS TCP TLS HTTP SERVER, current stop gold) at the top of the five stop scenes and a fixed vertical route (same five boxes, same place) in D, J and L; each stop got its own big picture (phone book, handshake arrows, lock and key, balancer fan, page card). `M.W` keys keep `%` and drop `.`: `0.999` is `0999`, `99.5%,` is `995%`. Check the spoken ORDER of two words before you hang a fade on one of them: "number comment likho" has `comment` after `number`, so a placeholder faded at `number` came back at `comment` (content_overlap at the end). A text swap in one card needs a 0.01 s fade on the old text, not `gone()`.
