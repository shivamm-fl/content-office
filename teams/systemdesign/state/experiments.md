# Experiments

What the team is trying and what came out of it. One block per experiment; any agent may add or close one. The Producer picks one experiment for each work day at the standup. Keep it short and honest:

Format:
- **<date> <name>**: hypothesis (what we think will happen and why). Reels: <ids>. We will know it worked when: <metric and rough size>. Result: <filled in later, with the numbers and how many reels>.

## Running

Reels: s006 (and s002). We will know it worked when: skip rate and avg watch of s006 vs s002 and the other four are compared once numbers are in (six reels, a hint only). Result: pending.
