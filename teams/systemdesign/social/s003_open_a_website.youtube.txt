What Happens When You Open a Website in Hinglish (Day 3/150)

When you type a web address and press enter, the browser looks up the server's IP address (DNS), sets up a connection, sends an HTTP GET request, receives the HTML, requests the images, styles and scripts the HTML refers to, and renders the page (MDN, How browsers work). The five-step grouping in this video is our own way of teaching it: for HTTPS sites the connection step also includes a secure (TLS) negotiation. The browser starts parsing HTML before all of it has arrived. The IP address 203.0.113.7 and the shopping-site picture are examples only. Explained with a courier-parcel analogy.
