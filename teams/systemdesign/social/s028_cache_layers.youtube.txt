Cache kahan rakhein: browser, CDN, app cache ya database?

Cache ek jagah nahi hota: same data browser, CDN, application cache aur database memory, in chaar jagah copy ho sakta hai. Jo layer user ke paas, wo fast par kam logon mein shared; aur copy stale ho sakti hai (max-age). Product photo ki request walk ek maana hua example hai.
