Day 1/150: Interviewer ne poocha, ek naya app design karna hai, sabse pehle kya karoge? Sabse pehle main sawal poochhunga.

System design matlab system ke parts aur unka aapas ka connection samajhna. System Design Primer ka suggested order: requirements, high-level design, har component ki detail, phir scale aur bottlenecks. Reel mein hum trade-offs bhi isi step mein dekhte hain. Ye koi rule nahi hai.

Example (invented numbers, apni arithmetic): ek food delivery app par 1,00,000 orders ek din mein, 86,400 seconds ka din, to average 1.16 orders per second. Menu cache se aaye to typically fast, par purana ho sakta hai. Database se aaye to fresh, par dheema.

Aapke liye sawal: 2,00,000 orders ek din mein hon, to average orders per second kitne? Jawab (apni arithmetic): 200000 / 86400 = 2.31 per second. Apna jawab comment karo.
