Day 6/150: Call mein awaaz katti hai, par download mein kyun nahi?

Internet par packet kho sakta hai. Tab do hi raaste hain: khoya tukda dobara bhejkar intzaar karo, ya chhodkar aage badho.

TCP pehle connection set karta hai, har packet ko number deta hai aur khoya packet dobara bhejta hai. File poori aur sahi order mein aati hai, bas thodi der se.
UDP koi taiyari nahi karta, packet bhejta hai aur dobara nahi bhejta. Isliye kisi ek tukde ke intzaar mein baaki cheez nahi rukti.

Faisla ek sawal se: der se aaya purana tukda kya ab bhi kaam ka hai? File mein haan, to TCP. Live call mein nahi, to UDP.

(10 packets aur 20 millisecond ke numbers sirf example hain. HTTP/3 UDP ke upar bana hai, isliye har website TCP par nahi hai.)
