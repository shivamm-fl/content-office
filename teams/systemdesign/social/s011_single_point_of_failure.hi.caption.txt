Day 11/150: ek hi server gira, aur poora app band ho gaya. Ye hota kyun hai?

Single point of failure (SPOF) yaani koi ek cheez jiska backup nahi, aur jiske girte hi sab ruk jaye.

3 steps:
1. Request ke raaste par har part se pucho: ye mara, to kya sab rukega? Haan hai, to wahi SPOF hai.
2. Uski ek aur copy lagao (redundancy). Load balancer dono ko dekhta hai, ek ruke to dusre ko bhejta hai (failover).
3. Jisne backup joda, use bhi jaancho. Akela load balancer hi naya SPOF ban jata hai.

Example: ek server 99% chalu, do aise servers dono ek saath girein sirf 1% x 1% = 0.01%, yaani 99.99%, saal mein lagbhag 53 minutes band. Ye tabhi sahi hai jab dono alag-alag girein; ek hi power socket par hon to dono ek gine jate hain. Aur har copy ka kharcha bhi badhta hai.

(99% wala server sirf example hai.)

Sawal: tumhare app ka sabse akela part kaun sa hai: server, database ya load balancer? Comment karo.
