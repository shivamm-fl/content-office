What Is System Design? The First Step in an Interview (Hinglish)

Day 1 of 150 Days of System Design. System design is about understanding a system's component parts and how they interact (Wikipedia). This video follows the System Design Primer's suggested approach: gather requirements and scale, outline a high-level design, dive into each component, then address bottlenecks and trade-offs, using an example food-delivery app (invented example numbers, our own arithmetic: 1,00,000 orders a day / 86,400 seconds = about 1.16 orders per second).
