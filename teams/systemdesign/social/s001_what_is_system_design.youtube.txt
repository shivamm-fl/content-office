What Is System Design? The First Step in an Interview (Hinglish)

Day 1 of 150 Days of System Design. System design means choosing the parts of a system and how they connect to meet requirements at a given scale. This video walks a commonly taught order for answering: requirements and scale, high-level design, component detail, trade-offs, using an example food-delivery app (invented numbers: 1,00,000 orders a day / 86,400 seconds = about 1.16 orders per second).
