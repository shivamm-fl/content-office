Day 10/150: 99.9% uptime ka matlab, saal mein kitni der band rahega?

Availability = uptime / total time. Har naya nine downtime ko lagbhag 10 guna kam kar deta hai.

99% = 3.65 din
99.9% = 8.76 ghante
99.99% = 52.6 minutes
99.999% = 5.26 minutes

Aur app ek akeli cheez nahi hai: load balancer, app server aur database ek ke baad ek chalte hain, to availability multiply hoti hai. Example: 0.999 x 0.999 x 0.999 = 0.997, yaani saal mein lagbhag 26 ghante band. Ye hisaab tab sahi hai jab parts alag fail hon aur har ek zaruri ho.

(Teen parts ke 99.9% sirf example hain.)
