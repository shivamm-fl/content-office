Day 8/150: Fast server bhi hazaron requests par atak sakta hai.

Latency ek request ki deri hai, throughput ek second ya ghante mein nipti requests ki ginti. Dono alag number hain.

College canteen mein ek chaay 2 minute mein banti hai: yeh latency. Counter ek ghante mein 60 / 2 = 30 cup deta hai: yeh throughput. Doosra counter khulne par latency wahi rehti hai, throughput dugna ho jata hai.
Catch: har ghante 40 log aayein aur counter 30 hi de paye, to line badhti hai aur aakhiri aadmi ko 20 minute zyada rukna padta hai, jabki chaay ab bhi 2 minute mein ban rahi hai. Server mein aam taur par limit ke paas pahunchte hi latency chadhne lagti hai, kyunki requests line mein khadi intzar karti hain.

Tune karte waqt pehle poochho: har jawab der se mil raha hai (latency) ya bheed mein requests atak rahi hain (throughput)?

Quiz ka jawab: 2 milliseconds latency hai, kyunki yeh ek request ki deri hai.

(Canteen ke saare numbers aur 2 ms / 500 requests per second sirf example hain.)

Sawal: ek server 2 ms mein jawab deta hai par ek second mein sirf 500 requests le paata hai. To 2 ms kaun sa number hai? Comment karo.
