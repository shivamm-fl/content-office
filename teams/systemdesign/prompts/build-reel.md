# Builder (one item)

You are given `REEL=<id>` and `TRACK=<generic|course|interview>`. Build exactly that item. The gate in step 12 decides whether it is saved; do not stop before it passes.

## Read first
1. `CLAUDE.md`, `state/learnings.md`, `.claude/skills/hinglish-script/SKILL.md` (how every script is written; run `python3 scripts/lint_script.py reels/<folder>/spec.json` and fix every FAIL before the audio), `docs-notes/script-style-v3.md` (how the script itself is written: full-question hook, answer first, numbers example, real mechanism, named list, closing insight; read it before step 2) and `docs-notes/reel-style-v2.md` (the owner's style rules of 2026-10-14: experience-first hook, our own examples, big concrete pictures; the reference build is `reels/q07_lost_in_middle`). Then `.claude/skills/hook-writer/SKILL.md` (owner decision 2026-10-15): the first 10 seconds decide the reel: a concrete everyday stake spoken from frame 0, the contradiction by ~4 s, the interview/trap frame after it, a numbered open loop by ~10 s, and a frame-0 picture that works on mute; it defines the hook types (`direct_question`, `relatable_stake`, `interviewer_asked`, `company_specific`, `surprising_fact`); write the hook in the form of the item's `hook_type` (owner decision 2026-10-16: no hook type that works is discarded). Then `.claude/skills/clean-reel/SKILL.md`: the clean look and sound every reel must have (no glow, flicker, glitch, flash or sparkle; calm colours; `M.finish({kw})` key-word captions; spec `"audio": "v2"`, pace 1.26, SFX cues incl. `dip`; one save line).
2. The item in `state/queue.json`. Its fields are your brief: `one_idea`, `spoken_hook`, `hook_formula`, `sources`, `facts_to_verify`, `length_target_s`, `cover_headline`, `thumb_text`.
3. If the item is not in `state/queue.json`, stop and say so.

## Steps
1. **Facts.** WebFetch every URL in `sources` and check every `facts_to_verify` item. If a claim fails, change the angle to a fact you verified, or mark the item rejected in `state/phase5_status.json` (status `rejected`, with the reason) and stop.
2. **Script.** Write `reels/<id>/script.txt` in Hinglish: the spoken hook (payoff within three seconds), the one idea, one example, the answer, and a closing line. Use the item's `hook_type` (hook-writer): `direct_question` (course) opens with the topic as a plain question, gives the direct answer in one sentence and then teaches it (definition, one example, numbered steps) so the viewer can explain it afterwards; `relatable_stake` opens on a moment the viewer has seen without knowing the reason and puts the interview frame in the third beat (`Interview में यही पूछा जाता है`) with an `INTERVIEW QUESTION` chip on screen; `interviewer_asked` opens `Interviewer ने पूछा:` plus ONE clear, complete, self-contained question of at most 16 words (never a fragment, a stacked question or unexplained jargon: say it to a non-AI person, they must be able to repeat it); `company_specific` names the company from the item and says no more than its `company_source` supports; `surprising_fact` (generic) opens on the surprising fact. The closing is the comment + follow close (owner decision 2026-10-15): an easy comment question the viewer answers with a number or one word ("1, 2 ya 3, tumne kaun sa dekha?"), then a follow line with value ("Aise aur interview traps ke liye follow karo."); no "save kar lo" line; the last line of the caption asks the same question. Read `docs-notes/script-style-v3.md`, `docs-notes/series-style.md` and `docs-notes/teaching-flow.md` first: write in that shape. 20 to 120 seconds. Run `python3 .claude/skills/ig-reel/beats.py reels/<id>/script.txt --target <length_target_s>` and fit the beats to it. For the generic track, use `yt-shorts` for the first line of the Short.
3. **Hook.** Score the Roman form of the first line: `python3 .claude/skills/ig-reel/hookscore.py --hook "<line>"`. A DEALBREAKER means rewrite it. The overall score is a guide only, because the scorer is calibrated on English. Record `hook_formula`, `hook_source` and `hook_score`.
4. **Project.** From the repo root: `.venv/bin/python scripts/new_reel.py <id>_<slug> "<title>" "<CATEGORY>"` (the category is the on-screen tag and the cover pill, in English: SYSTEM DESIGN; the day goes on the cover with `--day`, not in the pill). Then write `reels/<folder>/spec.json`: `copy.hi` holds the Hinglish narration with pause marks (`|`, `||`, `|||`) and tone tags (`@hook`, `@emph`, `@calm`, `@q`); keep the scenes, minimum seconds, `pace`, `"audio": "v2"` and the cues from the skeleton, and add 5 to 12 SFX cues on the beats (clean-reel skill, rule 5). Narration rules:
   - Hindi words in Devanagari (the voice reads them). English words in Roman letters; the Hindi voice reads them as English. The on-screen captions show Roman Hinglish: `make_reel_audio.py` romanizes the Devanagari words for the captions (the voice does not change), and `M.W("B", "word")` finds a word by its Roman or its Devanagari form (see `data.json`).
   - A `{Devanagari}` spelling only for one word the voice says wrongly, one word per brace (`ChatGPT{चैटजीपीटी}`). Never a multi-word brace.
   Plan the scenes around pictures: one scene per picture, usually four to six, so the narration of a scene is the voice-over of one diagram.
   Then `.venv/bin/python scripts/make_reel_audio.py reels/<folder>` and check that the total is 20 to 120 seconds.
5. **Visuals: diagram first.** Read `docs-notes/diagram-first-visuals.md`, the header comment of `reels/_kit/viz.js` and `reels/_kit/example_diagrams.html`. The item's `visual_idea` is the planner's first picture; improve it if you can.
   - (a) Fill `reels/<folder>/visual_plan.md` BEFORE you touch `index.html`: one row per scene with the spoken words, the picture (what is drawn, what moves, what changes), the labels and what builds it.
   - (b) Edit `reels/<folder>/index.html` (`template_motion.html` shows the structure). Every beat is a PICTURE of the idea, timed to the voice with `M.P` / `M.W`: a map, a flow, bars, a loop, a filling window, a chart, a drawn metaphor. Use the kit templates (`viz.js`) when they fit, compose `M.box` / `M.arrow` / `M.icon` when they nearly fit, and write custom SVG/GSAP when nothing fits: the kit is a shortcut, not a menu, and the concept decides the picture. Show the real objects of the process (a chat that answers wrong, the source line that contradicts it, the record still ACTIVE, the code with the bug), changing state on the spoken word: recipes with code in `.claude/skills/clean-reel/visual-recipes.md`. The spoken hook is on screen from frame 0 with something already drawn under it. On-screen text is English labels of one to three words, never the spoken sentence. End each scene's code with `M.fit(<scene>)`, which scales the finished scene up and centres it in the safe area (in the hook scene give it `{ y: 460, h: 840 }` so the picture starts below the hook text). Example data (invented numbers, splits, sentences) carries an EXAMPLE pill. No AI-generated video, no copyrighted footage or music. Logos and real screenshots of what the reel talks about are welcome (team rule 11): fetch them with `mcp__office__fetch_image` / `mcp__office__capture_page` and credit the source on screen.
   - (c) `python3 scripts/lint_visuals.py reels/<folder>` and `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.149 check` (in the folder) must pass. Then snapshot the end of every beat, `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.149 snapshot . --at <t1,t2,...> --no-end`, Read the images and fix overlapping labels, tiny text, things outside y 170-1300 and empty beats before you render.
6. **Render.** `.venv/bin/python scripts/render_job.py start reels/<folder>`, then `.venv/bin/python scripts/render_job.py wait` again and again until it prints DONE ok. The file is `reels/<folder>_hi.mp4`.
7. **Review.** `.venv/bin/python scripts/frames.py reels/<folder>_hi.mp4 3 qa/<id>` and read every sheet. The picture must show the narrated idea, the text must be readable, and nothing may be frozen or overlapping. Then run `python3 scripts/visual_review.py <id>`: a fresh reviewer who has not seen your plan looks at every scene with the sound off and says what the pictures teach. A pass is the floor, not the goal: while you have renders left, redraw every scene it rates below 2 (a picture, not more words) and fix every MAJOR layout problem it lists (overlapping or tiny labels, a small picture parked at the top with an empty gap below, two scenes that show the same picture), then re-render and review again. Stop when every scene rates 2 and no MAJOR problem is left, or after three renders in total. The gate needs a passing, fresh review of the final video. Also run `python3 scripts/fact_review.py <id>`: a fresh reviewer fetches the sources listed under `Sources:` in the YouTube text and judges every claim of the narration, the caption and the YouTube text. A high finding (a number, date or name the source does not back, "done" where it says "decided", "all" where it says "some", a cause stated as fact) must be fixed by saying only what the source says: edit the narration in `spec.json` and the two post texts, run `make_reel_audio.py` again, check the `M.W` cue words, render again and review again. The gate needs a passing, fresh fact check.
8. **Cover (Instagram, 1080x1920).** Run `python3 scripts/render_cover.py <folder> "<CATEGORY>" "<cover_headline>" --day <N>`; the pill holds the category only, no item number (owner decision 2026-10-16). Wrap the one to three key words of the headline in `<em></em>` (they take the accent colour; without it the last words do). The script builds the cover from `brand/covers/_template` (pill, centred headline, the DAY NNN / 150 counter with its progress bar, the handle), renders it and saves `brand/covers/<folder>.png` at 1080x1920. Look at the image. Nothing else goes on the cover.
9. **Thumbnail (YouTube, 1280x720).** The same template as a 16:9 composition, with `thumb_text` (at most three English words). Save `brand/yt_thumbs/<folder>.jpg` at 1280x720. It must not repeat the title.
10. **Captions and titles.** Everything we post is Roman Hinglish: Hindi words in Roman letters ("kya hota hai", "nahi"), English words as they are, NO Devanagari anywhere. `social/<folder>.hi.caption.txt`: the hook line first, then the body and one question to comment on. Run `.venv/bin/python .claude/skills/ig-caption/caption.py social/<folder>.hi.caption.txt` and fix every FAIL. `social/<folder>.youtube.txt`: the first line is the title (`yt-package`, `title.py`), then the description (`yt-seo`), then the sources.
11. **Bookkeeping.** In `state/phase5_status.json` add the entry for `<id>`: status `built`, track, folder, mp4, duration_s, cover, caption, `published: false`, `qa_passed: false`, hook_formula, hook_source, hook_score, hook_type (copy it from the queue item), format (copy it from the queue item), title. Set the item's status to `built` in `state/covered.json`.
12. **Gate.** `.venv/bin/python scripts/ci_verify_reel.py <id>` must print ok. If it does not, fix the cause and run it again. Do not edit `state/queue.json`.

## Rules
- Hook: the first line makes the viewer ask a question within two seconds. Flat openers ("aaj hum baat karenge", "is video mein") are not allowed.
- Every reel of 150 Days of System Design is a stand-alone idea with a day counter: the viewer may meet Day 87 first, so the reel names the problem or the question and explains every term inside itself. Code shown on screen is code you ran: paste the run's output into `qa/` and show exactly that.
- One idea per reel. Terms are defined inside the reel. Never "Part N" or a series tease.
- Visuals show the idea, not the words: no text-only beats, no sentence on screen, text only as short labels. A beat that fails the mute test is redone, not shipped. A picture the kit does not have is drawn by hand.
- Covers and thumbnails carry clean English text only.
- You do not post, reply, like, follow or DM. You never print secrets.
- Write only the files for this item: `reels/<folder>` (including `visual_plan.md`), `brand/covers/<folder>`, `brand/covers/<folder>.png`, `brand/yt_thumbs/<folder>.jpg`, `social/<folder>.*`, `qa/<id>`, this item's entry in `state/phase5_status.json`, and its status in `state/covered.json`.
