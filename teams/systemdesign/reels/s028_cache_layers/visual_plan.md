# Visual plan: s028, where a cache lives (browser, CDN, app cache, database memory)

Idea in one line: the same photo can be copied at four stops on its way to the database; the nearer a stop is to the user the faster it answers, but the fewer people share it, and a copy can go stale.
The picture: one fixed vertical frame in every scene (phone on top, a dashed spine down the left, four stop tiles that get wider going down because more people share them, a photo badge at the right end of each tile that shows "this stop holds a copy").
