# Visual plan: s024 Fixed window vs sliding window

Idea in one line: a fixed window starts a fresh count at every edge, so a client can fill the limit just before and just after the edge (2x in a short span); sliding window log (exact, one entry per request) and sliding window counter (two numbers, an estimate) fix it.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: ONE horizontal timeline (0 to 20 s) cut into two blocks by a gold edge line, a counter chip under each block, request squares piling up on the left and on the right of the edge with a bracket across it; then a gold window frame slides along the same timeline, old entries dropping out of its left side, and the count is recomputed live.
