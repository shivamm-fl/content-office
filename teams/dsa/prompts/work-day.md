Goal: the next day of 150 Days of DSA (the message gives the number, usually 1) built by the studio and waiting in the owner's inbox. The owner approves only finished reels: no plan or script approvals today. Ask the owner (`mcp__office__ask_owner`) only for a real blocker.

1b. **Standup (two minutes, in parallel, one short message each).** Ask the growth-analyst what moved since yesterday and what we should do differently today, and the strategist what is the one thing worth testing today. Decide one experiment for the day (a hook type, an ending, a way of opening) and add it as a line to `state/experiments.md` with the days it applies to and how we will know it worked. Close yesterday's experiment line if its numbers are in. Skip this only when there is nothing new since the last day.
2. **Pick today's days**: the oldest `planned` items in the queue that have no entry in `state/phase5_status.json` yet and are not already in the studio (the feed shows "Build <id>" conversations). If fewer than the number asked are planned, the strategist plans the next days of the roadmap in order with the researcher verifying every source (`prompts/plan-series.md`; `python scripts/validate_queue.py` must print ok). No plan approval is needed. Never skip a day: Day N+1 is not planned before Day N.
3. **Scripts.** The scriptwriter writes the script of each picked day (in parallel; hinglish-script, hook-writer, `lint_script`; the first spoken line at most 10 words), writing for the viewer first: the question the reel asks is answered early and clearly and the viewer leaves with something usable. Before a script goes on, the scriptwriter shows its hook and first lines to the growth-analyst for a two-line read and uses what helps. The researcher answers the scriptwriter's fact questions directly and runs any code that will be shown.
4. **To the studio.** For each reel call `mcp__office__request_build(item_id, item, script)` with the full queue item and the final script. The studio builds three at a time; the reviewers check each one and the finished reels go to the owner.
5. **Report** in at most six lines: the days (id, title) now in the studio, anything you could not do and why. Then finish; do not wait for the studio.
