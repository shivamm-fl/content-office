# Experiments

What the team is trying and what came out of it. One block per experiment; any agent may add or close one. The Producer picks one experiment for each work day at the standup. Keep it short and honest:

Format:
- **<date> <name>**: hypothesis (what we think will happen and why). Reels: <ids>. We will know it worked when: <metric and rough size>. Result: <filled in later, with the numbers and how many reels>.

## Running
- **2026-10-19 interviewer_asked opener on DSA**: opening with "Interviewer ne poocha: <question with a number>" gives lower skip rate than other openers (LearnAI: 69.7% vs 73.8-79.5%). Reels: d001. We will know it worked when: skip rate under 70% and avg watch above 7 s once the first reels have numbers (needs 3+ reels to compare). Result: pending.
- **2026-10-19 surprising_fact opener vs interviewer_asked (side by side)**: a number-led surprise ("Data dugna karo, search mein bas ek step badhta hai") holds viewers at least as well as d001's interviewer question, because it gives the payoff in the first line and teaches at once. Reels: d002 (against d001). We will know it worked when: skip rate and avg watch of d002 versus d001 once both have numbers (two reels are a hint, not proof; keep rotating hook types before judging). Result: pending.
- **2026-10-21 relatable_stake opener (third hook type)**: a stake-led opener ("Code atak raha tha, memory badhate hi chal gaya") followed straight by the example holds viewers like the other two types. Reels: d003 (against d001 interviewer_asked, d002 surprising_fact). We will know it worked when: skip rate and avg watch of d003 versus d001/d002 once numbers exist (three reels are a hint, not proof). Result: pending.
- **2026-10-22 direct_question opener (fourth hook type)**: a plain topic question with the answer in the very next line ("Array mein das lakhwa element, ek step mein kaise?") holds viewers like the other three types on a structure day. Reels: d004 (against d001 interviewer_asked, d002 surprising_fact, d003 relatable_stake). We will know it worked when: skip rate and avg watch of d004 versus d001 to d003 once numbers exist (four reels are a hint, not proof; day type differs, so do not conclude from one reel). Result: pending.
- **2026-10-23 wrong_way_first formula (relatable_stake, new formula)**: an opener that is the beginner's own failed attempt ("String ka ek letter badalne gaya, error aa gaya") with the real error in the next line holds viewers like the other four hooks on a structure day. Reels: d005 (against d001 to d004). We will know it worked when: skip rate and avg watch of d005 versus d001 to d004 once numbers exist (five reels are a hint, not proof). Result: pending.
- **2026-10-24 plain payoff-fact opener (surprising_fact, second use)**: a plain fact with no jargon ("Das lakh naamon mein ek naam, bina dhoondhe seedha mil jaata hai") holds viewers like the other hooks on a structure day. Reels: d006 (against d002 surprising_fact and d001, d003 to d005). We will know it worked when: skip rate and avg watch of d006 versus d002 and the rest once numbers exist (six reels are a hint, not proof). Result: pending.
