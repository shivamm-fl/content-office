Counting Bits | Blind 75 13/75

Given integer: 6. Output: [0, 1, 1, 2, 1, 2, 2].

count[i] = count[i >> 1] + (i & 1). Last bit hataane par smaller answer pehle se ready hai. O(n) time, O(n) output storage aur output ko chhodkar O(1) auxiliary space.
