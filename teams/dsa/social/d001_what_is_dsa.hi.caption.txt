Day 1/150: DSA kya hai? Data structure aur algorithm, 2 minute mein.

10 lakh sorted names mein ek name ek-ek karke dhoondho, to worst case mein 1,000,000 steps. Sorted list ko beech se aadha karte jao (binary search), to sirf 20. Maine Python mein chalaya: 1000000 vs 20. Shart: list sorted honi chahiye.

Data structure = data kaise rakha hai. Algorithm = us par steps.

Ab tum batao: 40 lakh names ho jayein, to halving mein kitne steps? Answer: 22. Pehle comment mein apna guess likho.
