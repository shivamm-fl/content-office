Two Sum Problem: O(n) with a Hash Map (Complement Lookup)

Two Sum: given an array and a target, return the indices of the two numbers whose sum is the target (exactly one answer, the same element is not used twice). Brute force checks every pair, O(n squared): 10 pairs for 5 numbers, about 50 million pair checks for 10,000 numbers (an example). With a hash map you compute complement = target - x, look it up in the numbers seen so far, and store x only after the check. One pass gives O(n) time and O(n) extra space with average O(1) lookups; the worst case for a hash map lookup is O(n), so O(n) overall is not strictly guaranteed.
