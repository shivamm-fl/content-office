Two Sum Explained: One Pass with a Hash Map

Two Sum: given a list of numbers and a target, find the two numbers that add up to the target and return their indices. Checking every pair is slow (10 checks on 5 numbers in our run; about 50 million pair checks for 10,000 numbers, an example). Instead, for each number look up its partner (target minus the number) in a hash map of numbers already seen, and store the number only after the check. One pass gives O(n) time and O(n) space, with average O(1) dictionary lookups.
