Day 11/150: Array ke kisi hisse ka sum, bina har baar loop chalaye?

Jawab hai prefix sum. Array ko ek baar chalakar running total bana lo, phir koi bhi range ka sum ek subtraction se milta hai: prefix[right + 1] - prefix[left].

Example array [3, 1, 4, 1, 5, 9]: prefix [0, 3, 4, 8, 9, 14, 23]. Index 2 se 4 tak ka sum 4 + 1 + 5 = 10, aur prefix se 14 - 4 = 10. Build ek baar O(n) mein hota hai, har query O(1), extra space O(n). Example ke liye 100000 numbers aur 100000 queries par worst case mein loop 10000000000 additions karta hai; prefix mein 100000 additions build ki aur 100000 subtractions queries ki.

Limit: ye trick sum par chalti hai kyunki sum ko subtraction se undo kar sakte hain. Max par nahi chalti.

Ab tumhara turn: prefix [0, 3, 4, 8, 9, 14, 23] hai, to index 1 se 3 tak ka sum kya hoga? Comment mein likho. Answer: 6 (9 - 3).
