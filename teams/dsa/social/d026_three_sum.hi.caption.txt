Day 26/150: 3Sum: duplicate triplets ke bina zero sum wale teen numbers kaise nikaaloge

3Sum mein array se aise teen numbers chahiye jinka sum zero ho, aur har triplet sirf ek baar aana chahiye. Brute force teen loops se har triple dekhta hai: ek hazaar numbers par 166 million se zyada triples, yaani O(n^3). Sort karo, ek index i fix karo, aur baaki array par Two Sum II ke do pointers chalao: sum kam ho to l aage, zyada ho to r peeche, zero ho to triplet likho aur dono pointers andar. Duplicates ke liye i par, aur hit ke baad l aur r par, barabar values skip karo. Skip ke bina fifty zeros par 600 triplets nikalte hain, sahi answer ek hai. Time O(n^2), output chhodkar extra space O(1), sort ka internal stack gino to O(log n). Actual Python code video mein hai (code chalaya).
