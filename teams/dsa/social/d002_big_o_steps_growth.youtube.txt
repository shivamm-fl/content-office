Big-O Explained in Hinglish: O(n), O(log n), O(n squared)

Big-O describes how the number of steps an algorithm needs grows when the input grows. Python run, worst case, target last (counts, not seconds): one by one 1000000 steps for 10 lakh names and 2000000 for 20 lakh; halving a sorted list 20 and 21; checking every pair 499500 steps for 1000 names and 1999000 for 2000. Halving needs a sorted list. "Steps, not seconds" is our own framing.
