Blind 75 16/75: Climbing Stairs

Given integer: 5 stairs. One-step ya two-step moves -> 8 ways.

Last move ke disjoint cases: ways[i - 1] + ways[i - 2]. Zero stairs ka one empty route base hai. Previous two counts simultaneously update hote hain. O(n) time, O(1) extra space.

Dono counts ek saath update karna kyun zaroori hai?
