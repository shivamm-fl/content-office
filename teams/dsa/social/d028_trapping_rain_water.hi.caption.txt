Day 28/150: Trapping Rain Water, bina extra array ke, ek pass mein

Har bar par paani = min(left max, right max) - height. Array 4, 2, 0, 3, 2, 5 par total 9 units paani rukta hai. Brute force har bar ke liye dono taraf poora array scan karta hai: 10,000 bars par lagbhag 100 million steps, yaani O(n^2). Left max aur right max ke do arrays pehle se bana lo to time O(n), par extra space bhi O(n).

Trick: formula mein min hai, to sirf chhoti side maayne rakhti hai. Do pointers lo, jis side ki height chhoti ho usi side ka paani jodo aur wahi pointer andar karo. Time O(n), extra space O(1). Actual Python code video mein hai (code chalaya, random arrays par brute force se match kiya).
