Day 7/150: Interviewer ne poocha: do naam ek hi jagah rakhne pade, to kya karoge?

Jab do key ek hi slot par aa jaati hain, use collision kehte hain. Chhota example (sirf example): key ko 8 se bhaag do, jo bache wahi slot. 10, 18 aur 26 teeno slot 2 par aate hain.

Pehla tarika chaining: har slot mein ek chhoti list. 26 ko dhoondhne mein 3 comparisons lage (maine chalakar dekha). Doosra tarika open addressing: ek slot mein ek hi cheez, slot bhara ho to agla khaali slot dekho, ise linear probing kehte hain.

Agar bahut saari keys kuch hi slots mein jama ho jaayein (jaise 1000 keys, sab 8 ke gunaj), to ek lookup mein 1000 comparisons tak lag sakte hain. Isiliye hash map ka lookup average O(1) hai, worst case O(n). O(1) tabhi, jab keys slots mein phaili hon.

Ab tum batao: hamari chaining wali table mein key 27 dhoondhni ho, to kitne comparisons lagenge? Comment mein likho. Answer: 2 comparisons, kyunki slot 3 ki list [3, 27] hai.
