Container With Most Water In Python: Two Pointers, O(n) Time, O(1) Space

Container With Most Water (LeetCode 11): pick two walls so the water between them is largest; area is the shorter height times the gap. Brute force checks every pair, O(n^2): for n = 1000 that is 499,500 pairs. Start with the widest container, l at the first wall and r at the last, and always move the pointer at the shorter wall inward: with that wall fixed, any narrower container is no larger, so the wall can be dropped (our own derivation). On [1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7] this takes 8 steps and finds 49. Moving the taller wall instead returns 8 on the same array. Time is O(n), extra space O(1) (the same approach as the doocs solution for LeetCode 11). In our own run the two-pointer code matched a brute-force check on 5,000 random arrays.
