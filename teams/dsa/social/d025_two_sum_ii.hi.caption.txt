Day 25/150: Two Sum II: sorted array mein extra space ke bina pair kaise dhoondhein

Two Sum II mein array sorted hai, answer 1-indexed chahiye aur extra space constant (O(1)) rehni chahiye. Two Sum wala hash map yahan allowed nahi, kyunki wo n entries store karta hai. Har number ke liye binary search O(1) space deta hai, par time O(n log n). Sorted hone ka fayda lo: do pointers, l shuru par, r aakhir par. Sum kam ho to l aage, zyada ho to r peeche, har step ek index hamesha ke liye hat jaata hai. Time O(n), extra space O(1). Return l+1, r+1 yaad rakho. Ye code unsorted array par kaam nahi karta.
