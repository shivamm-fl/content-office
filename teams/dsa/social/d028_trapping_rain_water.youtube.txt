Trapping Rain Water In Python: Two Pointers, O(n) Time, O(1) Space

Trapping Rain Water (LeetCode 42): water above a bar = min(highest bar on the left, highest bar on the right) - its height. For [4, 2, 0, 3, 2, 5] the answer is 9. Brute force scans both sides for every bar, O(n^2). Prefix and suffix max arrays give O(n) time with O(n) extra space. Two pointers keep O(n) time and O(1) space: the side with the smaller height is settled by its own running max, so add its water and move that pointer in. In our own run the code matched a brute-force check on 20,000 random arrays.
