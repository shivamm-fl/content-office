A: AI se photo maangi, usne sirf likhkar de diya, kyon? Kyunki wo text model tha. AI model ek cheez nahi hoti, aur ye uske input aur output se tay hota hai.
B: Mixer aur toaster dono bijli se chalte hain, par mixer mein bread toast nahi hoti. AI mein bhi aise hi 4 kinds hain: text, image, audio aur embedding.
C: Pehla, text model. Text andar, text bahar. Tum sawaal likhte ho, wo jawab likhta hai.
D: Doosra, image model. Andar likha hua prompt, bahar picture. Photo isi ka kaam hai.
E: Teesra, audio model. Ye do taraf chalta hai: awaaz andar, transcript bahar, ise speech recognition kehte hain. Ya text andar, awaaz bahar.
F: Chautha, embedding model, aur ye sabse alag hai. Embedding yaani text ka numbers ki list mein badla hua roop, jo matlab naapta hai, words nahi. Maan lo ek note mein likha hai "refund", aur tumne khoja "paise wapas". Words ek bhi nahi milte, par inke numbers ek-doosre ke paas baithte hain.
G: Ek asli number: OpenAI ka text-embedding-3-small ek sentence ko 1,536 numbers mein badalta hai. Ye vendor ka apna aankda hai.
H: Ab dekho asli app mein ye kaise judte hain. User likhta hai "paise wapas". Embedding model sahi note dhoondhta hai, aur text model usse jawab likhta hai.
I: Isliye jo chatbot tumhe photo deta dikhta hai, wo aam taur par peeche kisi image model ko bula raha hota hai. Akela chat model sab nahi kar sakta.
J: To yaad rakho: andar kya jata hai aur bahar kya aata hai, wahi model ki kind hai.
K: Ab tum batao: voice note ko text mein badalna ho, to kaun sa model chahiye? Comment mein likho, jawab caption mein hai. Roz ek AI idea ke liye follow kar lo.
