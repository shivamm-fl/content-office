A: ChatGPT ne language kaise seekhi? Bahut saara text padhkar! Is seekhne ko kehte hain pretraining. Aaj dekhenge ki text padhkar model seekhta kaise hai.
B: Socho, ek kitab hai jisme har line ka aakhri word chhupa hai: the dog sat on the... Tum guess karte ho, phir dekhte ho: rug. Jawab kisi ne alag se likha nahi, wo text mein hi chhapa hai. Model yahi karta hai, bas kitabein bahut saari.
C: Pretraining yaani model ko shuru se train karna. Shuru mein uske andar ke numbers, yaani weights, random hote hain. Use language ka kuch nahi pata. Dekhne ke liye maine Python mein ek chhota toy model chalaya, asli LLM nahi, bas ginti karne wala. Ye EXAMPLE hai: mera banaya text, sirf 53 words ka, jisme 12 alag words hain.
D: Pehla step: text ka agla word chhupa do. Doosra step: model guess karta hai, har word ko ek chance dekar. Teesra step: asli word kholo aur naapo ki guess kitna galat tha. Is galti ke score ko kehte hain loss. Loss jitna kam, guess utna achha. Chautha step: model apne numbers thode badalta hai, taaki wahi word agli baar zyada likely lage. Koi insaan label nahi lagata, text khud hi answer sheet hai.
E: Ab chart dekho. Neeche model ne kitne words padhe, upar uska loss, yaani guess ki galti. Kuch padhne se pehle use koi andaza nahi, to 12 words par barabar chance. Loss 3.58. 10 words padhne ke baad 3.09. 20 ke baad 2.74. 40 ke baad 2.37. Poora text padha, to 2.19. Line har baar neeche aayi: 3.58 se 2.19. Matlab jitna zyada padha, guess utna behtar. Jaise the ke baad ab wo cat ya dog ko sabse likely manta hai, kyunki dono 4-4 baar aaye the.
F: Loss zero nahi hua, kyunki the ke baad kai words sahi ho sakte hain.
G: Asli LLM mein ginti ki table nahi, neural network hota hai, aur wo tokens, yaani text ke tukdon par chalta hai. Par kaam wahi: agla token guess karo, loss naapo, numbers badlo.
H: Aur pretraining ke baad model bas text aage badhana jaanta hai, abhi wo sawal-jawab wala assistant nahi bana. Uske liye baad mein fine-tuning hoti hai, insaanon ke labels wale examples se.
