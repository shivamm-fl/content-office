Onit ke AI Engineer job post mein agent loops hain. Loop mein phans jaye, to kaise rokoge? Jawab 3 rules mein hai. Galti ye hai ki model par chhod do, wo khud tay kare kab rukna hai. Tab tokens jalte rahenge. Pehla rule, stopping condition. Anthropic kehta hai, maximum number of iterations jaisi conditions aam hain. Maan lo max 10 steps, uske baad loop kat jaye. Koi fixed number wo nahi deta, task dekhkar chuno. Doosra rule, ground truth. Har step par environment se asli nateeja lo, jaise tool result, taaki agent jaane ki wo sach mein aage badh raha hai. Teesra rule, human checkpoint. Checkpoints par ya atakne par, agent human feedback ke liye pause kare. Agent ko rukna bhi sikhana padta hai. Tum max steps kitne rakhoge? 5, 10 ya 20? Comment mein likho. Aur interview sawalon ke liye follow karo.
