40 page ka handbook, aur usne RAG bana diya. Use RAG ki zaroorat hi nahi thi. Interview mein yahi poocha jata hai: poora PDF prompt mein daalun ya RAG banaun? Iske 3 rules hain. Galti ye hai ki har PDF par seedha RAG laga dete hain, jabki chunking aur retrieval dono toot sakte hain. Pehla rule, size dekho. Anthropic kehta hai, knowledge base 200,000 tokens se chhoti hai, yani kareeb 500 pages, to poori prompt mein daal do. Doosra rule, prompt caching lagao. Anthropic ke apne figures mein, latency 2x se zyada kam, aur cost 90 percent tak kam. Teesra rule, usse badi ho, tab RAG: sirf relevant chunks retrieve karo. Pehle fit karke dekho, phir retrieve karo. Tumhara sabse bada PDF kitne page ka hai? 40, 500 ya zyada? Comment mein likho. Aur interview sawalon ke liye follow karo.
