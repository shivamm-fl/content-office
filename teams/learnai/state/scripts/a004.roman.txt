A: ChatGPT jawab kaise likhta hai? | Ek-ek word guess karke! || Tumhare phone ka keyboard bhi yahi karta hai. | Aaj dekhenge ki ye guess asal mein hota kaise hai.
B: Phone mein Good likho, to keyboard upar kuch words suggest karta hai: | morning, night, luck. || Wo agla word guess kar raha hai. || ChatGPT jaise models ko kehte hain LLM, yaani large language model. | Aur ye bhi yahi karte hain, bas bahut bade paimane par.
C: Ise saaf dekhne ke liye maine ek chhota toy model banaya. | Asli LLM nahi, bas ginti karne wala. || Ye EXAMPLE hai: | maine apne banaye 8 chhote sentences diye, | jaise the cat sat on the mat, the cat sat on the sofa. || Inmein sat on the 6 baar aaya. || Har baar uske baad kaun sa word aaya, model ne gin liya.
D: Ab sentence hai: the cat sat on the. | Agla word kya hoga? || Pehla step: model saare possible next words ki list banata hai, har ek ke saath odds, yaani chance. || 6 mein se 3 baar mat aaya. | To mat ka chance 50 percent. || sofa 2 baar: 33 percent. || bed 1 baar: 17 percent. || Teeno milakar 100 percent.
E: Doosra step: is list mein se ek word chuno. | Maine sabse upar wala chuna: mat. || Teesra step: use sentence mein jod do. | Ab sentence hai: the cat sat on the mat. || Aur yahi naya sentence wapas model ko jaata hai, | agla word poochne ke liye. || List banao, ek chuno, jodo, phir se. | Isi loop se jawab banta hai, ek-ek word karke.
F: Asli LLM mein ginti ki table nahi hoti, usmein neural network hota hai. | Aur wo words par nahi, tokens par kaam karta hai. || Token yaani text ka tukda: | ek poora word, word ka hissa, ya ek character bhi. || Lekin kaam wahi hai: | probabilities ki list, ek chuno, jod do, repeat.
G: Ek baat yaad rakhna: | sabse likely word aur sahi fact ek cheez nahi hai. || Model dekhta hai ki aage kya aana likely hai, | ye nahi ki kya sach hai.
H: To ChatGPT ka har jawab aise banta hai: list, chuno, jodo. || Ab batao, agar model hamesha sabse upar wala word hi chune, | to kya jawab har baar same aayega? || Comment karo: SAME ya DIFFERENT. || 150 Days of AI ke liye follow kar lo.
