A: Interviewer ne poocha: prompt mein examples dene se, model hamesha behtar jawab deta hai? Tumhara jawab hona chahiye: hamesha nahi. Examples jawab ki shakl dikhate hain, par galat chune, to nuksan bhi karte hain. 3 steps mein samjhata hoon.
B: Log sochte hain, jitne zyada examples, utna behtar. Maan lo review ko positive ya negative batana hai. Bina example ke prompt 23 tokens ka hai. Token yaani text ka ek chhota tukda, aur bill inhin ka banta hai. 5 examples jodo, to wahi prompt 85 tokens ka ho jata hai. Har request par 62 tokens zyada.
C: Pehla step: examples se jawab ki shakl dikhao. Label yaani sahi jawab ka naam, jaise positive ya negative. Examples dikha den ki jawab inhin mein se ek ho, to model bhatakta nahi, kyonki wo agla shabd andaze se banata hai. Ek research mein 12 models par labels random kar diye, phir bhi performance kam giri. Maayne format aur label ke options rakhte hain.
D: Doosra step: har tarah ke case rakho, kyonki model examples ki aadat pakad leta hai. Teenon examples positive hon, to use lag sakta hai ki jawab hamesha positive hai. GPT-3 par examples aur unka order badalne se, accuracy andaze se lekar best results tak badal gayi.
E: Teesra step: apne data par naapo, kyonki kaun sa example madad kar raha hai, ye andaze se pata nahi chalta. 0, 3, 5 examples ke saath accuracy naapo, aur jahan fark ruk jaye, vahin ruk jaao.
F: Ek boundary: reasoning models, yaani jo jawab se pehle khud sochte hain, unmen aksar examples nahi chahiye. Pehle bina examples try karo.
G: Interview mein aise bolo: examples jawab ka format sikhate hain, isliye 3 se 5 alag-alag examples se shuru karo. Phir bina examples ke prompt se tulna karo, aur fark na dikhe, to examples mat badhao.
H: Ab tum batao: teenon examples positive hon, to model kis taraf jhuk sakta hai? Positive ya negative, comment mein likho. Roz ek naya interview sawal, follow kar lo.
