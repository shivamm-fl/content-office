Chatbot ne bola: aaj mausam bahut namkin hai. Interview mein isi par sawal aata hai: temperature, top-k aur top-p, teenon ka kaam kya hai? tumhara jawab hona chahiye: teenon ke kaam alag hain. do options ki list chhoti karte hain, ek bache hue options ke chance badalta hai. 3 steps mein dekhte hain. Model agla word aise chunta hai: "aaj mausam bahut" ke baad har word ko ek probability, yaani chune jaane ka chance. maan lo 8 words mein, sabse upar achha, 37 percent, sabse neeche namkin, 0.6 percent. ab temperature, yaani model kitna jokhim le, use badhakar 1.5 kar do. namkin ka chance teen guna, 1.9 percent. koi word hata nahi. pahla step, top-k. Top-k yaani sirf upar ke k words rakho. k equals 3 rakha, to bache achha, garam aur thanda, namkin bahar. kyonki jo hat gaya, wo ab kabhi chuna hi nahi jaaega. doosra step, top-p. Top-p mein ginti nahi, kul chance dekhte hain. upar se words jodte jaao, jab kul 90 percent paar ho jaye, ruk jaao. chauthe word tak 89 percent tha, to paanchvan bhi liya. kyonki model ko bharosa ho to kam words bachte hain, uljha ho to zyada. teesra step, temperature. Temperature list nahi kaatta, bache hue words ke chance badalta hai. 0.5 par achha 37 percent se badhkar 56 percent, 1.5 par ghatkar 29 percent. kyonki kam temperature bade chance ko aur bada karta hai. teenon saath lagao, to ek ka asar doosre par padta hai. Top-p 0.9 par 5 words bache the, temperature 1.5 karte hi 6 bache. kyonki chance bant gaye, to 90 percent tak zyada words lagte hain. Interview mein ye bolna: top-k ginti se aur top-p kul chance se list kaatte hain. Temperature kuch nahi kaatta, chance badalta hai. teenon saath hon, to ek badalte hi doosre ka asar badal jata hai. ab tum batao: temperature badhane se betuke words hat jate hain, ya aane ke chance badhte hain? hatte hain ya badhte hain, comment mein likho. roz ek naya interview sawal: follow karo.
