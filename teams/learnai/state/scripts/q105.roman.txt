Interviewer ne poocha: LLM mein Temperature, Top-K aur Top-P ka difference batao. Bhai, teeno AI ko next word choose karne mein help karte hain... par teeno ka kaam alag hai! Maan lo AI ko complete karna hai: Aaj mausam bahut... AI ke paas options hain: achha, garam, thanda... aur namkeen! Ab game samjho. Top-K bolta hai: sirf top 3 probable words rakho. Achha, garam, thanda — bas! Namkeen ki probability kitni bhi ho, agar woh top 3 mein nahi hai, toh out! Top-P fixed number nahi dekhta. Ye highest-probability words ko add karta hai, jab tak unki cumulative probability threshold tak nahi pahunch jaati. Model confident hai toh kam options. Probability spread hai toh zyada options ho sakte hain. Temperature ka kaam alag hai! Low temperature? High-probability words ko aur preference. High temperature? Probability distribution flatter, toh less-likely words ko bhi comparatively zyada chance. Matlab temperature badhao, toh namkeen jaisa word bhi zyada likely ho sakta hai — guaranteed nahi! Interview mein yaad rakhna: Top-K = fixed number of candidates. Top-P = cumulative probability ke basis par candidates. Temperature = probability distribution kitni sharp ya flat hogi. Ab batao: temperature badhane se namkeen word delete hoga ya uska relative chance badh sakta hai? Comment karo!
