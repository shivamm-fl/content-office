Model agla word chunne se pehle har option ko ek score deta hai. || "Mera favourite khel hai" ke baad maan lo: cricket 2, football 1, chess 0.5, hockey 0. | Ye numbers example hain, real model ke nahi.
Pehla step: har score ko T se divide karo. | T yaani temperature ka number. || Doosra step: scores ko percent mein badlo, taaki kul sau bane. || Teesra step: un percent ke hisaab se dice ghumao, aur ek word chuno.
