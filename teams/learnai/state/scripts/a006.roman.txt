A: ChatGPT ko 'namaste dost' kaise dikhta hai? Model letters nahi dekhta, use sirf numbers dikhte hain. 'namaste dost' ke liye ye teen numbers hain: 12682, 5642, aur 49415. Ye kaise bante hain, dekhte hain.
B: Railway ticket counter socho. Tum station ka naam bolte ho, clerk tumhe ek number wala ticket deta hai, aur system sirf wo number padhta hai. AI model ke saamne bhi aisa hi ek counter hai. Use tokenizer kehte hain, yaani wo hissa jo text ko numbers mein badalta hai.
C: Pehle socho, sabse aasaan tareeka kya hoga? Har letter ko ek number de do. Par 'unbelievable' mein 12 letters hain, to model ko 12 numbers padhne padenge, ek chhote se word ke liye. Aur har poore word ko ek number dein, to koi naya word aate hi sab atak jayega. Isliye teesra raasta: text ko chhote tukdon mein kaato. In tukdon ko token kehte hain.
D: Pehla step: kaatna. 'namaste dost' teen tokens mein kata: nam, aste, aur dost, jiske aage space bhi juda hai. Token poora word bhi ho sakta hai, word ka hissa bhi, punctuation bhi. Kyun? Kyunki tukde dobara kaam aate hain, to list chhoti rehti hai.
E: Doosra step: lookup. Tokenizer ke paas ek fixed list hai, jise vocabulary kehte hain. Isme har tukde ka apna number hai, yaani id. Maine OpenAI ka tiktoken chalaya, encoding cl100k_base. Isme 100,277 tokens hain. nam ka id 12682, aste ka 5642, dost ka 49415. Common word poora rehta hai: 'Hello world' sirf 2 tokens hain.
F: Teesra step: model sirf ye numbers leta hai, aur jawab bhi numbers mein deta hai. Aakhir mein tokenizer un numbers ko wapas text bana deta hai. Maine decode karke dekha: text bilkul wahi wapas aaya.
G: Ab ek galatfehmi. Log sochte hain, token yaani word. Nahi. 'strawberry' ke 10 letters hain, par wo 3 tokens mein kata: str, aw, berry. Model ko ye teen numbers milte hain: 496, 675 aur 15717, ten letters nahi. Aur ye numbers sirf isi tokenizer ke hain. Doosre models text ko alag tarah se kaatte hain.
H: To yaad rakho: tokenizer text ko tokens mein kaatta hai, vocabulary se har token ka id nikalta hai, model sirf ids dekhta hai, aur aakhir mein ids wapas text bante hain.
