# Visual plan: q107 (question 105, version B), interview: temperature, top-k and top-p (relatable_stake, @ai.learnvora)

Idea in one line:
Three settings that look alike do three different jobs: top-k and top-p cut the list of next words (by count and by total chance), temperature only reshapes the chances of the words that are left, and changing one changes what another keeps.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: one toy list of 8 candidate words drawn as bars with their real chances (the nonsense word NAMKIN at 0.6 percent); raising temperature makes the bars flatter and NAMKIN three times likelier, but removes nothing; a cut line after word 3 (top-k) removes NAMKIN for good; a running total that stops at 90 percent after 5 words (top-p); the same top-p keeps 6 words once the bars are flatter.

Look: clean-reel (flat navy, gold accent; cyan = a bar, red only for the nonsense word and a loss). Header: AI ENGINEER INTERVIEW. EXAMPLE pill on the invented chat and the invented word chances; every number is from `toy_run.py` (seeded, printed in the notes).
