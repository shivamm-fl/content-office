# Visual plan: a017 How AI builds an answer

One running example, "The sky", grows by one piece per step. The percentages and pieces are invented teaching numbers (EXAMPLE pill, and an INVENTED NUMBERS label while the voice says so). Gold marks the picked piece, cyan the options and text, red only a stop. Labels are 1-3 words.

| Scene | Spoken (short) | The picture | Labels | Built with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Does AI write the whole answer at once? No, piece by piece | Under the hook an ANSWER card with three dashed slots; tiles "The sky", "is", "blue" land one after another; a red ALL AT ONCE mark flips to a green ONE PIECE AT A TIME | ANSWER, ALL AT ONCE, ONE PIECE AT A TIME | M.hook, custom card and tiles |
| B | Complete "The sky...", options is 70 / was 30, invented numbers | A TEXT SO FAR card with "The sky..." above a NEXT PIECE? card with two true-proportion bars | EXAMPLE, TEXT SO FAR, NEXT PIECE?, INVENTED NUMBERS | custom bars |
| C | is picked, text becomes "The sky is", it is the next input | The STEP 1 card rings "is" and dims "was"; the "is" tile joins the TEXT strip; a big arrow drops into an INPUT OF STEP 2 card | STEP 1, TEXT, INPUT OF STEP 2 | custom card, strip |
| D | blue 60 / clear 40, blue picked, "The sky is blue", the loop | A STEP 2 card with bars; blue is ringed; the TEXT strip gets a gold "blue" tile; a LOOP card with a big ↻ closes | STEP 2, TEXT, LOOP, PICK → ADD → REPEAT | custom card, strip |
| E | A token is a word, part of a word or punctuation; stops on stop token or limit | Three rows (sky / play + ing / .) land one by one, then a red [STOP] tile and a bar that fills to its end for the limit | EXAMPLE, WORD, PART OF A WORD, PUNCTUATION, STOP TOKEN, OUTPUT LIMIT | custom rows |
| F | Interview recap: options, pick and add, repeat | Three numbered cards: mini 70/30 bars; "The sky" + "is" tiles; a ↻ beside "The sky is blue" | OPTIONS, PICK + ADD, REPEAT | custom cards |
| G | After blue, does the next step see "The sky" or "The sky is blue"? Comment, follow | The text "The sky is blue" above two answer buttons; a comment bar; the handle card with FOLLOW | CHECK YOURSELF, NEXT INPUT?, COMMENT, FOLLOW | custom cards |
