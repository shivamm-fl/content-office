# Visual plan: a018 Temperature: The Randomness Dial

One running example: next word after "Mera favourite khel hai ..." with four options (cricket, football, chess, hockey). Scores and percentages are toy numbers (EXAMPLE pill, INVENTED NUMBERS label), computed in `state/runs/a018_temperature_run_output.txt`. The same four option rows return in scenes C, E and H so the viewer sees one object change. Gold marks cricket and the key value, cyan the other options, red only a wrong word, green only the right one. Labels are 1-3 words.

| Scene | Spoken (short) | The picture | Labels | Built with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Same question, why a different answer each time? | Under the hook a SAME PROMPT card "Mera favourite khel hai ..." and three RUN cards that land one by one with three different words: cricket, hockey, football | SAME PROMPT, RUN 1-3 | M.hook, custom cards |
| B | Strict friend always cricket, relaxed friend sometimes football; that looseness is temperature; the model did not change its mind | A question bubble, a STRICT FRIEND row of three cricket tiles, a RELAXED FRIEND row (cricket, football, cricket), then a gauge whose needle swings from low to high under a TEMPERATURE pill that turns into SAME MODEL | STRICT FRIEND, RELAXED FRIEND, TEMPERATURE, SAME MODEL, T: LOW / T: HIGH | custom gauge and tiles |
| C | The model gives each option a score: cricket 2, football 1, chess 0.5, hockey 0 (invented) | The prompt card above four option rows; on each spoken word the row gets a bar of true relative length and its score | EXAMPLE, SCORE, INVENTED NUMBERS | custom rows |
| D | Step 1 divide by T, step 2 percent that make 100, step 3 roll a die and pick one word | Three numbered cards: tiles "2 ÷ T, 1 ÷ T, 0.5 ÷ T, 0 ÷ T" with T gold; four growing bars and "= 100%"; a die that rolls and a "1 WORD" tile | DIVIDE BY T, PERCENT, DICE, T = TEMPERATURE | custom cards |
| E | Turn the dial: T 0.2 gives cricket 99.3 %, T 1 gives 57.9 / 21.3 so football appears, T 2 gives 40.9 and bars nearly equal | A gauge with a T readout next to a 3 TRIES panel; the four option rows below re-compute softmax live as T moves through 0.2, 1, 2 (real percentages); the picks panel shows cricket three times, then cricket / football / cricket, then chess / cricket / hockey; football and hockey rows are ringed when named | T = 0.2, NEXT WORD, 3 TRIES | custom gauge, rows, softmax tween |
| F | High T is not always good: wrong word in facts or code, so keep T low there; a bit higher for ideas and stories | FACTS · CODE card: gauge high, code line "total = a + b" whose operator turns into a red "-" (WRONG WORD), then the gauge swings low and it returns to green "+" (RIGHT WORD); an IDEAS · STORIES card with a medium gauge and a story line | FACTS · CODE, WRONG WORD, RIGHT WORD, IDEAS · STORIES, MORE VARIETY | custom gauge, code line |
| G | Interview: model makes percent, a die picks a word; temperature sharpens or flattens the percent | A PERCENT → DICE → 1 WORD flow, then two bar groups: LOW T · SHARP (one tall bar) and HIGH T · FLAT (four similar bars) with true heights | INTERVIEW, PERCENT, DICE, 1 WORD, LOW T · SHARP, HIGH T · FLAT | custom flow and bars |
| H | What was cricket's percent at T 2? Comment, follow | The T = 2 option rows with the cricket number hidden as "?", a COMMENT bar and the handle card with FOLLOW | CHECK YOURSELF, T = 2, COMMENT, FOLLOW | custom rows, cards |
