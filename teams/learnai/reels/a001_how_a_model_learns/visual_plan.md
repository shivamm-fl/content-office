# Visual plan: Day 1, how models learn (a001, @ai.learnvora), owner script, 167 s preview

Idea in one line:
A model does not memorise answers: it learns like a basketball shot (shot, miss, understand the mistake, try again) with maths, and the same loop runs on one tiny model (forward pass, loss, gradient, update, repeat) until the miss is small.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: a crossed-out stack of question-answer cards; a ball misses a hoop; three hours-to-marks cards and one dial called weight; the gap per card squared and averaged into a loss gauge; the slope of a loss curve (the gradient) rolling the dot down; a bigger learning rate sending the dot off the chart; a field of billions of dials with the loop pipeline; the closed loop and a check question.

Look: clean-reel (flat navy, gold accent, red only for a miss or a wrong value, cyan for neutral parts). Header: 150 DAYS OF AI, DAY 1. The STEP n/3 kicker first appears in scene C.
