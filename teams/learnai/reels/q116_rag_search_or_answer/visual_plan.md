# Visual plan: q116 RAG wrong answer, search or answer? (rules: docs-notes/diagram-first-visuals.md)

Idea in one line: when a RAG answer is wrong, look at the chunks the search handed over, not at the answer: right chunk missing means search failed, right chunk there but answer wrong means the writing step failed, and you fix only that part.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: ONE pipeline QUESTION, SEARCH, CHUNKS, ANSWER with the wrong bubble at the end, then a magnifier on the chunk strip, then a test table with two columns of ticks and crosses that sorts every wrong answer into one of two causes.
