# Visual plan: a013 The transformer: the design behind every LLM

Idea in one line:
A transformer is one block (attention, then a small network per word) stacked many times; one block holds about 3.15 million numbers, one third attention and two thirds feed-forward; six blocks in the 2017 paper.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: three name cards point into one box; the box becomes a block that splits into ATTENTION and FEED-FORWARD; a sentence with lines from one word to the others; three words each through their own small network; a bar split 1/3 and 2/3 under a 3,152,384 counter; six blocks stacked with a token travelling up; same-design blocks with different numbers; a recap and a 10-block quiz. The numbers are our count (OUR COUNT pill).
