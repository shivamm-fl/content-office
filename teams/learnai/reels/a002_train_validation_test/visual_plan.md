# Visual plan: Day 2, train, validation and test: three piles of data (a002, @ai.learnvora)

Idea in one line:
Data is split into three piles with three different jobs: the model learns from train, we choose between models on validation, and the test pile is opened once at the very end, because only data the model has never seen tells how it will do on new data.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: a textbook, a mock test and a final exam turn into three piles of dots; a 1,000-dot grid is cut into three blocks of true size (70, 15, 15 percent); a model that memorises scores 100 and drops to 72 on dots it has not seen; a second model scores 82 on both; the pile opens once and shows 79; peeking at the test score and changing the model turns the green pile gold.

Look: clean-reel (flat navy, gold accent; cyan = train, gold = validation, green = test, red only for a miss or a wrong value). Header: 150 DAYS OF AI, DAY 2. EXAMPLE pill on the invented 1,000 emails; the accuracy numbers come from a small run on fake data (seed 36, median of 200 seeds is 100/73/72 and 81/81/79).
