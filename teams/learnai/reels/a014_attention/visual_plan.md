# Visual plan: a014 Attention (how a word looks at the other words)

Idea in one line: the same word "bank" gets a different meaning because it weighs its neighbours; one picture (a column of the sentence's words, bank at the bottom, curved lines to the others whose thickness is the weight) carries all four steps.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: two phrases with the word bank in gold from frame 0 (river = waves, loan = coins), then one sentence column where bank's lines to its neighbours get thick or thin, percent bars add to 100, and a water/money meter fills; the second sentence flips the thick line to loan and the meter to money.
