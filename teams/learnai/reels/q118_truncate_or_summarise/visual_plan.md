# Visual plan: q118 Truncate or summarise a long chat

Idea in one line: truncating cuts the oldest messages and loses the fact the user said first; summarising folds the old part into a short note that keeps it, and the last few messages stay word for word.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: ONE chat stack of 12 message bars (bar height = words, true scale) inside a dashed WINDOW with a LIMIT 60 line. Truncate: the top three bars (the first holds a red "peanut allergy") slide out over the line, the counter drops 78 to 57, a red stamp says ALLERGY GONE and the bot answers wrong. Summarise: bars 1 to 8 fold into one gold note card that still lists the allergy, the last 4 bars get a gold ring, the counter reads 38 and there is FREE ROOM in the window.
