# Visual plan: q114 metadata filter (rules: docs-notes/diagram-first-visuals.md)

Idea in one line: filter the search by the metadata the question already names (year), then watch the approximate index scan leave only about 4 of 40 candidates, so use exact search or iterative scans.
The picture that explains it with the sound off: a shelf of document cards tagged 2022/2023/2024 where the 2022 row is blocked and 2024 kept; later a 40-card candidate strip where a 10 percent bar leaves 4 green cards.
