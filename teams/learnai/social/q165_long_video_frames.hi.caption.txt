Ek ghante ki video, AI beech ka scene bhool gaya. Interview mein poochte hain: ek ghante ki video AI model ko kaise doge?

Jawab: har frame mat bhejo.
Step 1: transcript padho, wahi se pal milta hai.
Step 2: poori video se kuch frames sample karo (Gemini default 1 frame per second, 5 second mein 1 bhi chalta hai).
Step 3: us pal ka 30 second ka clip kaato aur uspar zyada fps se zoom karo.

EXAMPLE (maan liye numbers): 1 ghanta, 30 fps, 66 tokens per frame. Har frame = 7,128,000 tokens. Sample + zoom = 57,420 tokens, lagbhag 124 guna kam.
Tez action par 1 fps mein detail kho jaati hai, isliye us clip par fps badhao.

Interview mein bolo: main poori video nahi bhejta, transcript se pal dhoondhta hoon, kam frames sample karta hoon, aur fps sirf chhote clip par badhata hoon.

Tumhari video mein zyada kaam ki cheez kya hai, boli hui baat ya dikhne wali action? Comment karo.

Source: Google Gemini API docs (video understanding). Transcript + sampling ka tareeka humari apni salah hai.
