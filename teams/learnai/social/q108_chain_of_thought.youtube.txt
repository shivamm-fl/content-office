Chain-of-thought prompting hamesha kaam karta hai? AI interview jawab

Interview mein poochha jata hai: kya chain-of-thought prompting (model se step by step solve karne ko kehna) hamesha performance badhata hai? Jawab: nahi. Complex, multi-step problems mein ye madad kar sakta hai, jaise shop bill ka invented example jahan steps likhne se calculation ki galti pakadi ja sakti hai. Simple question par ye sirf output tokens, latency aur API cost badhata hai. Reasoning models pehle se multi-step reasoning ke liye designed ho sakte hain, isliye unhe "think step by step" kehna redundant ho sakta hai. Interview mein bolne wala jawab: dono approaches ko representative examples par test karo aur accuracy, latency, cost compare karo.

Is video mein: chain-of-thought prompting, think step by step, tokens, reasoning models, prompt engineering interview. Check yourself ka jawab: TEST FIRST.
