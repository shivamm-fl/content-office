Day 13/150: ChatGPT, Claude, Llama ka design ek hai. Kaun sa?

Transformer: attention par bana design. Har block mein pehle attention aata hai (words ko context ke saath jodta hai), phir feed-forward network (har token ko alag se process karta hai).

Example: "Ravi ne chai banayi, aur use pee liya." "Use" Ravi hai ya chai? Clue "pee liya" hai, isliye attention chai ko bada weight deta hai (0.8 aur 0.1 sirf example numbers hain). Feed-forward mein 512 numbers ko 2048 tak expand karte hain, negative ko 0 karte hain, phir wapas 512 (original base model ke sizes). Chhota example: x aur minus x, ReLU lagao, to -3 aur 3 dono ka jawab 3: seedhi line ye nahi kar sakti.

Ab tum batao: "Ravi ne gilas giraya, aur wo toot gaya." "Wo" kiske liye hai? Jawab: gilas, clue "toot gaya": gilas tootta hai, Ravi nahi.

Comment mein apna attention ka example likho.
