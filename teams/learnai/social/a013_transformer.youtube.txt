The transformer: the design behind every LLM

Transformer kya hota hai? Is video mein dekhte hain attention aur feed-forward network ek example sentence par kaise kaam karte hain, 512 se 2048 tak expand karne ka matlab kya hai, aur blocks ka stack kaise banta hai.

Is video mein: what is a transformer in AI, transformer architecture explained, attention is all you need, how LLM works, AI engineer roadmap.

Transformer ka idea (sirf attention par bana network), base model ke sizes (6 layers, d_model 512, d_ff 2048), feed-forward ka ReLU wala formula, decoder ke masked attention aur encoder-decoder attention Vaswani et al. 2017 ke paper se hain. Ravi aur chai wala sentence, 0.8 aur 0.1 weights, aur x aur minus x ka example hamare apne illustrative examples hain, kisi model ke asli numbers nahi.
