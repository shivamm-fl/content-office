Day 2/150: Train, validation aur test data alag kyun rakhte hain?

Jaise tum exam ki taiyari karte ho: textbook se practice, mock test se kamzori ki jaanch, aur final exam mein aise questions jo tumne pehle kabhi nahi dekhe. AI model ka data bhi aise hi 3 piles mein baanta jata hai.

Example: 1,000 emails (spam ya nahi), 700 train, 150 validation, 150 test.
Train: model isi se seekhta hai.
Validation: isse hum jaanchte hain ki model kahan kamzor hai aur do models mein se kaun sa chunna hai.
Test: final exam, sirf ek baar kholte hain.

Chhote nakli data par do models chalaye. Jo model train data rat leta hai, uska accuracy train par 100%, validation par 72%. Jo sirf pattern pakadta hai, uska accuracy train aur validation dono par 82%, aur test par 79%. Training ka score bharose layak nahi hota, test ka number hota hai.

Agar test ka score dekhkar model badal diya, to test ab test nahi raha, wo bhi validation ban gaya.

Check yourself: test ka score dekhkar model badal do, to kya test score par bharosa kar sakte ho? Haan ya nahi, comment mein likho.
