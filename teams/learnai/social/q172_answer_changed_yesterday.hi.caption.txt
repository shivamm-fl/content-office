Meta ke interview mein poocha gaya: sahi jawab kal badal gaya, par training data mein purana jawab bhara pada hai. Sahi jawab kaise doge?

Example: company ne return window 30 days se 15 days kar di. Purane blogs aur FAQs mein har jagah 30 likha hai, model ne wahi padha hai, to woh 30 hi bolega.

Badalne wali jaankari model ki memory se mat lo. Naya policy page retrieve karo aur prompt mein date ke saath do. Trap: model saaf evidence maan leta hai, par context mein kahin purani baat bhi padi ho to woh usi ko pakadta hai jo pehle se maanta hai (confirmation bias). Isliye purane versions index se hatao ya expired mark karo, prompt mein likho ki nayi date wala context sahi hai, aur badle hue facts ka alag test set rakho.

Purana blog aur naya policy page dono retrieve ho gaye. Model ko kaise pata chalega kise maanna hai? Comment karo.

Source: "Adaptive Chameleon or Stubborn Sloth" (arXiv 2305.13300). Return window wala case example hai.
