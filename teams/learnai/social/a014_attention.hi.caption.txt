Day 14/150: River bank aur bank loan: model farak kaise jaanta hai?

Word wahi hai, bank. Par ek mein matlab kinara hai, doosre mein paise wala bank. Attention yahi suljhata hai: har word baaki words ko dekhta hai aur tay karta hai ki kisko kitna matlab dena hai. Chaar steps: query (bank ka sawal), key (har word ka label), softmax (scores ko percent banao, jo milkar 100 hon), value (percent se guna karke jod do).

Ye numbers humne example ke liye banaye hain. Asli model inhe khud seekhta hai.

Ab tum batao: river ko 77 percent, bank khud ko 13 percent, baaki chaar words ko milakar kitna? Comment mein likho. Jawab: 10 percent.
