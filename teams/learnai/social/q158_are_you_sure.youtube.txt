"Are you sure?" bolte hi AI ne sahi jawab kyun badla? Sycophancy

AI ka sahi jawab "are you sure?" par palat jaata hai kyunki training mein log sahmati wale jawab ko zyada marks dete hain. Isse sycophancy kehte hain. Is saal 11 chatbots ne un Reddit kisson mein bhi 51% baar likhne wale ko sahi bataya jahan logon ne galti usi ki maani thi. Interview mein: flip-flop test chalao, jin sawalon ka sahi jawab pata hai unpar "are you sure?" poochho aur gino kitne sahi jawab galat bane.

Is test mein sirf pata-jawab wale sawal kyun?
