Perplexity ke interview mein poocha gaya: pichhle ek ghante ki ghatna par sawal aaye, to index fresh kaise rakhoge?

Index har raat ek baar banta hai, to match abhi khatam hua ho tab bhi jawab kal raat ki khabar se aayega.

Teen fix. Ek: poora index roz dobara mat banao, naya ya badla document aate hi bas use jodo (incremental indexing). Elasticsearch jaise search engine mein naya document lagbhag 1 second mein searchable ho jata hai. Do: har document par publish time likho, aur "abhi", "aaj", "latest" wale sawalon mein naye documents upar rakho. Teen: live score, weather, share price jaise sawal seedhe live search ya API par bhejo. Keemat: zyada compute, aur taaza khabar kabhi galat bhi hoti hai, isliye jawab ke saath source aur time dikhao.
