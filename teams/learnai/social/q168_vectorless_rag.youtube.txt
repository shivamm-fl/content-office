Vectorless RAG Kya Hai? PageIndex Bina Vector DB

Traditional RAG document ke chunks banata hai, embeddings nikalta hai aur similarity search se chunks dhoondhta hai. PageIndex (VectifyAI) document ka tree index banata hai aur LLM khud reason karke sahi section chunta hai: no embeddings, no vector database, phir bhi RAG. Example: 500 pages ki assumed annual report aur sawal ki company loss mein kyun gayi. Ye PageIndex ke apne claims hain.
