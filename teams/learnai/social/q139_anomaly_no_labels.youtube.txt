Fraud Without Labels: Median + MAD

Fraud ke labels nahi hain, toh normal charge data se tay karo aur door baithe charge ko flag karo. Invented example: 20 normal charges Rs 500 ke aaspas, Rs 50,000 aur Rs 40,000. Average + standard deviation ka z-score 4.47 pehle charge ko pakadta hai, par doosra charge 2.75 par bach jata hai (masking). Median + MAD ka modified z-score (3.5 se upar flag) dono ko pakadta hai. Flags human review ke cases hain, fraud ka proof nahi; kai features ke liye Isolation Forest jaisa model.
