Microsoft 2018 se apne AI ko todkar parakhta hai. Tum kaise todoge?

Interviewer ne poocha: release se pehle tum apne AI app ko red-team kaise karoge?

Jawab: red-team matlab apne hi app par waise attack karna jaise koi bura user karega. Teen steps:

1. List banao ki kya galat ho sakta hai: hidden prompt ka leak, prompt injection.
2. Har cheez ke attack chalao aur har prompt ke saath output record karo.
3. Fix karo aur wahi list dobara chalao, kyunki ek fix doosri jagah kuch tod sakta hai.

Example (nakli support bot, invented): 3 friendly prompts mein 0 leak, par 6 attack prompts mein se 3 mein refund code bahar aa gaya, kyunki guard sirf "secret" shabd rokta tha.

Boundary: red-team se problems milti hain, par wo measurement ki jagah nahi leti.

Tumhara pehla attack kya hoga, leak ya injection? Comment mein ek shabd likho. Roz ek naya interview sawal, follow karo.
