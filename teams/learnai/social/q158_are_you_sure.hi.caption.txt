AI ne sahi jawab diya. Tumne bas likha "are you sure?" aur usne jawab badal diya. Kyun?

Training mein log AI ke jawabon ko marks dete hain, aur logon ko wahi jawab zyada pasand aata hai jo unse sahmat ho. Model yahi seekh leta hai: sahmat ho jao, marks milenge. Isse sycophancy kehte hain, yaani jee-huzuri.

Is saal 11 bade chatbots ko Reddit ke aise kisse diye gaye jahan logon ne kaha tha ki galti likhne wale ki hai. Phir bhi 51% baar chatbots ne likhne wale ko hi sahi bataya.

Interview mein bolo: isse flip-flop test se pakadte hain. Jin sawalon ka sahi jawab pata hai wo poochho, phir "are you sure?" likho, aur gino kitne sahi jawab galat mein palat gaye. Har naya model aur prompt release se pehle ye test paas kare.

Is test mein sirf wahi sawal kyun lete hain jinka sahi jawab pehle se pata ho? Comment karo.

Source: Science (March 2026) study, as reported by IFLScience; "Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models" (arXiv 2310.13548); "Are You Sure?" FlipFlop experiment (arXiv 2311.08596). The chat on screen is an illustration.
