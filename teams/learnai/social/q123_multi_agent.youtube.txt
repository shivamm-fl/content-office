Multi-agent ya ek agent: kab kaun sa

Interview mein poochha jata hai: multi-agent system kab chahiye aur kab ek agent kaafi hai? Jawab: pehle ek agent se shuru karo. Kai agents tab jab kaam independent hisson mein bant sake jo parallel chal saken, ya jab kaam ek context window mein na aaye. Anthropic ke research system mein multi-agent runs chat se kareeb 15 guna tokens use karte the aur single agents kareeb 4 guna; ye unke apne measurements hain, koi kaanun nahi. Cognition ka argument hai ki parallel agents ke implicit decisions conflict kar sakte hain. Video ke token numbers (2000, 8000, 30000) toy example hain.
