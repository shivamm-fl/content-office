Accenture ke LLM interview mein poocha gaya: Generative AI aur Agentic AI mein fark kya hai?

Generative AI prompt se content banata hai. Flight cancel hui to woh refund request likh dega, par airline ki site kholna, form bharna, status dekhna tumhe karna hai.

Agentic AI ko goal do, to LLM ek loop mein chalta hai: socho, tool chalao, natija dekho, phir socho, jab tak goal poora na ho. Agla step LLM khud chunta hai.

Follow-up bhi aata hai: kya har app agent ho? Agent mehnga aur dheema hai, aur ek step ki galti aage badhti hai. Steps pehle se pata hon to fixed workflow, varna agent.

AI se email likhvana, ya AI ka khud tumhare inbox se email bhej dena: agentic kaun sa hai, aur kyun? Comment karo.

Source: candidate ka interview experience (GeeksforGeeks, Accenture LLM Operations Engineer, Dec 2025) aur Anthropic, "Building effective agents".
