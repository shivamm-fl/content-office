AI ne doosre AI ka dimaag khola aur use khud theek kar diya. Training zero.

MIT CSAIL researchers (2 Nov 2026) ne GPT-6 Astra ko Qwen3.5-0.8B ke weights padhne diye. Qwen ek hi baat baar-baar dohraata tha. Astra ne 2 layers mein us loop ka pattern dhoondha aur hata diya: sirf 1,31,072 weights, yaani model ka 0.016%. Unke test mein MATH-500 score 7.9% se 48.1%.

Isse kehte hain weight-space programming: ek AI, doosre AI ke weights khud padhta aur likhta hai. Limit bhi hai: ye rukawat hata sakta hai, nayi kaabiliyat nahi daal sakta.

Agar AI, AI ko khud theek karne lage, to AI engineers ka kaam kya hoga? Comment karo.
