ChatGPT ko pretrain karte vaqt labels kisne lagaye?

Pretraining mein koi human labeler nahi hota: model text ka agla word chhupakar guess karta hai, aur text khud hi answer key hai. Is video mein ek chhota toy counting model chalta hai (example, invented 53 words ka text, 12 unique words, asli LLM nahi): loss, yaani error score, 3.58 se 2.19 tak girta hai jaise model zyada text padhta hai. Phir dekhte hain ki real LLM mein billions of transformer parameters hote hain, aur helpful assistant banane ke liye human labels baad mein fine-tuning mein aate hain.

Is video mein: pretraining kya hai, self-supervised learning, loss, next word prediction, fine-tuning.

Toy model ke numbers hamare apne example ke hain, kisi source ka claim nahi. Pretraining ki definition (random weights se shuru, raw text par, self-supervised, next word prediction ek example, fine-tuning baad mein human labels ke saath) Hugging Face LLM course se hai.
