AI model seekhta kaise hai? Loss aur gradient descent

AI model basketball seekhne jaisa seekhta hai: guess karo, naapo kitna galat tha (loss), aur weight ko gradient ke ulte taraf thoda sa khiskao. Is video mein ek weight wala chhota example hai (study hours se marks), jisme hum khud chalaye numbers dikhate hain: learning rate 0.1 par w 4 updates mein 2 par converge hota hai, aur learning rate 0.5 par loss badhta jata hai (diverge). Wahi loop badi LLMs ko train karta hai, jinme billions of parameters hote hain (sabse bade models mein 100 billion se zyada, jaise GPT-3 ke 175 billion).

Is video mein: forward pass, loss function, gradient descent, learning rate. Check yourself ka jawab: 3.
