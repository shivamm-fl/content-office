Context compaction kya hota hai? Lambi chat ki summary banakar nayi window shuru karna.

Jab chat window ki limit ke paas pahunche, to teen steps chalte hain:
1. Summarize: decisions aur unresolved bugs rakhkar chhoti summary
2. Restart: nayi window mein sirf wahi summary
3. Prune: redundant tool outputs hatao, kyunki tokens badhne par model ka recall ghatta hai
