Interviewer ne pucha: AI app ne galat jawab diya, to galti kahan hui, kaise pata lagaoge?

Galti: sirf aakhiri jawab dekhna. Langfuse ke docs ke hisaab se trace ek request ki poori life ka record hai: LLM calls, retrieval steps aur tool executions, timing, input aur output ke saath.
1. Har request ka ek trace banao
2. Har step ka input, output, timing aur token usage rakho, courier tracking jaisa
3. Galat jawab ka trace kholo aur pehla galat step dhundho
Example: refund chatbot ne 30 din bola, trace mein dikha ki search ne purana policy page uthaya. Tab prompt badalna bekar tha.
