Same question, different RAG answers: how to make it consistent (NVIDIA AI interview)

Inconsistency has three sources: sampling, retrieval and model version. Lower randomness where the setting exists, log the retrieved chunk ids for every answer, pin a fixed model snapshot and run evals before changing it, and cache the approved answer for frequent questions. The probability bars in the video are an illustration (temperature 1 vs 2, computed).
