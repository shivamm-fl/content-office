Vector database ki jagah AI khud document mein search kar sakta hai? Interview mein yahi poochte hain: vectorless RAG kya hota hai?

Traditional RAG: PDF ke chunks, embeddings, similarity search. Problem: similar ka matlab hamesha relevant nahi, aur chunking se context toot sakta hai.
PageIndex (VectifyAI) ka tareeka: document ka hierarchical tree (headings, summaries, page range) banao aur LLM khud reason kare ki kaun sa section kholna hai.
Matlab no embeddings, no vector database, phir bhi RAG. Isse vectorless, reasoning-based RAG kehte hain.

EXAMPLE (maan liye): 500 pages ki annual report, sawal: company loss mein kyun gayi.

Tumhare hisaab se kis document pe vectorless RAG zyada kaam karega? Comment karo.
