Multi-agent mein alag context kyun: interview jawab

Interview mein poochha jata hai: multi-agent system mein har agent ka context alag kyun rakhte hain? Jawab: Anthropic ke research system mein subagents apni alag context windows mein parallel kaam karte hain aur lead ko sirf condensed findings lautate hain, isliye lead ka context chhota rehta hai. Numbers (12000, 3000, 4000, 5000 aur 200 words) invented example hain. Anthropic ne apne data mein dekha ki multi-agent systems chats se karib 15x zyada tokens use karte hain (June 2025). Cognition (12 June 2025) ke mutabiq jab subagents ek-doosre ke kaam ko nahi dekhte, to unke implicit faisle takra sakte hain.
