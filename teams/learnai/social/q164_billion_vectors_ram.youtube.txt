1 billion vectors, 128 GB RAM: kaise fit karoge?

Raw data lagbhag 3 TB hai. Product quantization se har vector 64 bytes (+8 ka ID, lagbhag 72 GB), IVF se query sirf 32 / 65,536 groups mein (lagbhag 5 lakh vectors), aur top 100 ko SSD par poore vectors se rerank. DiskANN ne 64 GB RAM + SSD par 1 billion points 3 ms se kam mein search dikhaya.
