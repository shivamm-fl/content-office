Galti: sab model par chhod dena ki wo khud tay kare kab rukna hai. Tab tokens jalte rahenge. Anthropic ke building effective agents post ke hisaab se teen rules:
1. Stopping condition, jaise maximum number of iterations (example: max 10 steps)
2. Ground truth: har step par environment se asli result, jaise tool result
3. Human checkpoint: checkpoints par ya atakne par agent human feedback ke liye pause kare
