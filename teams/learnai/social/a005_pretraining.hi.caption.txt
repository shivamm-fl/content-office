Day 5/150: Interviewer ne poocha: ChatGPT ko pretrain karte vaqt dataset par labels kisne lagaye?

Zyaadatar log bolte hain humans, aur yahi reject hote hain. Pretraining mein koi human labeler nahi hota: text khud hi answer key hai.

Mechanism simple hai: sentence ka agla word chhupa do ("the dog sat on the...") aur model ko guess karne do. Hamne ek chhota toy model chalaya (example, asli LLM nahi): 53 words ka text, 12 unique words. Shuru mein weights random hote hain, to 12 words par equal probability aur loss (error score) 3.58.

Phir cycle: guess, real word reveal, loss calculate, weights update. 10 words ke baad loss 3.09, 20 par 2.74, 40 par 2.37, poora text padhne ke baad 2.19. Loss zero kabhi nahi hota, kyunki ek word ke baad kai options valid hote hain.

Real LLM mein counting table ki jagah billions of transformer parameters hote hain, par rule wahi hai: next token predict karo, loss calculate karo, weights adjust karo. Is stage par model sirf text complete karna jaanta hai; helpful assistant banane ke liye human labels baad mein fine-tuning mein aate hain.
