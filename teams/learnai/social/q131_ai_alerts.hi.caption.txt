Raat 2 baje users bole jawab galat, aur dashboard green tha. Kaun se alerts lagaoge?

Interview mein poochha jata hai: production mein AI app ke liye kaun se alerts lagaoge?

Jawab: sirf errors nahi, chaar tarah ke alerts, kyunki AI app galat jawab dekar bhi error nahi dikhata.
1. Classic signals: latency, traffic, errors, saturation (Google SRE book ke four golden signals).
2. Cost: tokens ka bill, roz ka kharch dekho.
3. Quality: roz kuch asli jawab eval se check karo, pass rate gire to alert.
4. Safety: guardrail ya refusal achanak bahut badhe to alert.
Phone sirf us par bajao jo urgent ho aur jis par tum kuch kar sako. Baaki dashboard par.

Example (invented hafta): errors 1% ke aaspas rahe, 5% ka alert kabhi nahi baja. Par jawab ka pass rate 90 se 78 par aaya, aur 80% ke neeche ka alert paanchve din se bajta. Quality, cost aur safety alerts hamari recommended practice hain, kisi standard ka quote nahi.

Tumhare app mein pehle kaun sa alert lagaoge: quality ya cost? Comment mein likho. Roz ek naya interview sawal, follow karo.
