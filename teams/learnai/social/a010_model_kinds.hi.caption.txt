Day 10/150: AI se photo maangi, usne sirf likhkar de diya, kyun?

Kyunki wo text model tha. Model kaun sa hai, ye is se tay hota hai ki andar kya jaata hai aur bahar kya aata hai.
1. Text model: text andar, text bahar.
2. Image model: likha hua prompt andar, picture bahar.
3. Audio model: awaaz andar, transcript bahar, ya ulta: text andar, awaaz bahar.
4. Embedding model: text andar, numbers ki list bahar, jo matlab naapti hai. Isiliye "paise vaapas" khojo to "refund" wala note mil jaata hai, bhale ek word na mile.

Asli app mein ye sab peeche jude hote hain, isiliye photo maangne par app aam taur par image model ko bulata hai.

Ab tum batao: voice note ko text banana ho, to kaun sa model? Comment mein likho, jawab: audio model (speech to text).

Roz ek AI idea ke liye follow karo.
