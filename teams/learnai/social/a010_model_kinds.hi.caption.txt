Day 10/150: AI se photo maangi, usne sirf likhkar de diya, kyun?

Kyunki wo text model tha. Model kaun sa hai, ye is se tay hota hai ki input kya hai aur output kya hai.
1. Text model: text input, text output.
2. Image model: likha hua prompt input, picture output.
3. Audio model: awaaz input, transcript output, ya ulta: text input, awaaz output.
4. Embedding model: text input, numbers ki list output, jo matlab measure karti hai. Isiliye "paise vaapas" khojo to "refund" wala note mil jaata hai, bhale ek word na mile.

Asli app mein ye sab peeche jude hote hain, isiliye photo maangne par app aam taur par image model ko bulata hai.

Ab tum batao: voice note ko text banana ho, to kaun sa model? Comment mein likho, jawab: audio model (speech to text).

Roz ek AI idea ke liye follow karo.
