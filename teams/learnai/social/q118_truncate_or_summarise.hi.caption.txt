Pehle message mein peanut allergy batayi, bot bhool gaya. Interview mein poochha jata hai: lambi chat chhoti karni ho to truncate karoge ya summarise? Jawab: purane hisse ka chhota note (summary) banao, aakhiri kuch messages word for word rakho, aur truncate sirf tab jab purane messages mein kaam ka kuch na ho.

Example invented hai, words tokens ki jagah hain. 12 messages ki chat, kul 78 words, window mein sirf 60 aate hain. Truncate mein 9 messages bachte hain aur allergy wala pehla message chala jata hai. Summary (14 words) aur last 4 messages milake 38 words hote hain, aur allergy bachi rehti hai.

Teen steps: 1) Purane hisse ka summary banao. 2) Aakhiri 4 messages word for word rakho. 3) Allergy jaise fact ko pin karo, taaki summary ki summary mein detail na khoye. Boundary: summary ke liye extra model call lagti hai aur detail galat ho sakti hai, isliye purane messages mein bas "hi, hello" ho to truncate theek hai.

Interview mein bolo: "Truncation silently drops the oldest messages, where the user often says the one fact that matters. I summarise the old part, pin key facts, and keep the last few messages word for word."
