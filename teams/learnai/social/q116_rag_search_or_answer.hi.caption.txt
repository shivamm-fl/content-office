Interviewer ne poocha: RAG ka jawab galat aaye, to kaise pakdoge ki galti retrieval ki hai ya generation ki?

Final answer mat dekho, pehle dekho ki search ne kaun se chunks diye. Sahi chunk nahi mila, to retrieval fail. Mila phir bhi jawab galat, to generation fail.

Teen steps: 1) Har galat jawab ke liye woh chunks nikalo jo model ko bheje gaye the. 2) Har question ka sahi chunk pehle se tay rakho aur dekho woh top 2 mein aaya ya nahi. 3) Sirf wahi hissa theek karo jo fail hua: retrieval fail to search ya chunking, generation fail to prompt ya model.

Example (invented shop documents, measured nahi): 6 test questions mein se 5 mein sahi chunk top 2 mein aaya. 3 galat jawabon mein 1 retrieval ki galti thi, 2 generation ki.

Interview mein bolo: "Main final answer nahi, pehle retrieved chunks dekhta hoon. Sahi chunk nahi mila to retrieval theek karta hoon, mila phir bhi jawab galat to generation."
