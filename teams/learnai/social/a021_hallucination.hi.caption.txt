Day 21/150: AI ne nakli kitab ke 312 pages bataye, kyun?

Model bas agla sabse likely word chunta hai, aur us step mein koi facts check nahi karta. Isliye jab use jawab nahi pata, tab bhi naam ya number wala smooth, confident jawab ban sakta hai. Ise hallucination kehte hain. Anthropic ki guide ke teen tareeke: model ko "I don't know" bolne ka rasta do, apne documents do aur har baat ke saath quote maango (quote nahi mila to claim hata do), aur wahi sawal kai baar poochho, alag jawab matlab pakka nahi. Ye hallucination ko kam karte hain, khatam nahi. Reel ki book The Tin Lantern aur percent numbers example hain, real model ke nahi.
