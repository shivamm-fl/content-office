Chhota jawab padhne se bill kam hota hai?

cc-stretch ek Claude Code plugin hai jo reply ko chhote skim view mein dikhata hai, aur underlined phrase par click karne se wo wahin khul jata hai (stretchtext). Author ke hisaab se 69 replies mein skim view sirf 17% words ka tha (author ke apne numbers). Claude har level pehle likh deta hai, isliye reply ke tokens lagbhag utne hi lagte hain: author ke shabdon mein, aap kam padhte ho, par kam pay nahi karte. Plugin Anthropic se juda nahi hai, early access plugin API par bana hai, terminal mein click ke liye /tui fullscreen chahiye, aur Windows par click abhi nahi chalta.

Is video mein: plugin kya karta hai, bill kyun nahi ghatta, aur setup.
