7B model on a laptop: quantization

Example: 16 GB laptop, 7B model. Weight memory = parameters x bytes per weight: fp16 14 GB, int8 7 GB, int4 3.5 GB (weights only, arithmetic, KV cache needs extra memory). Quantization stores weights in lower precision to cut memory; fewer bits can cost some accuracy, so test the quantized model on your own task.
