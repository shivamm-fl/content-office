Top-k vs top-p: how an LLM trims its shortlist

Day 19/150: before picking the next word, a language model can cut its options to a shortlist. Top-k keeps a fixed number of the most likely words. Top-p (nucleus sampling) keeps the smallest group of words whose chances add up to p, so the shortlist size adapts to how sure the model is. The percentages in the reel are invented toy numbers, not from a real model.
