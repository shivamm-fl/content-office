# LearnAI team

LearnAI teaches AI to Hindi and Hinglish speakers who want to become AI engineers, on Instagram (@ai.learnvora, renamed by the owner from ig.learnai on 2026-10-18; every new video, caption and cover uses the new handle), Facebook (Learnai.ai) and YouTube (LearnAI). This folder is the team's workspace in the content office: the owner starts the work day from the office app, approves the finished reels (and any script they ask to see), and posts from the Calendar tab. Agents never post.

## The goal
**Grow the handle**: more people who watch a reel to the end, save it, share it and follow, week after week. That is the team's single goal and the measure of every choice. You are a small reel team that owns this result, not stations of a pipeline: talk to each other, ask each other early, challenge each other's work, try things, keep what works and write down what you learned (`state/learnings.md`, `state/team_playbook.md`, `state/experiments.md`). Every day the team should be a little better than the day before. **Value first (company rule 8): every reel teaches; hooks and growth tuning never cost a reel its clarity or value, and nothing filler sits between the hook and the first line that teaches.**

| Track | Ids in the office | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| series | a001, a002 ... | 150 Days of AI: one idea a day in the order of `roadmap/series-150-draft.json`. Live, 1 a day. |
| interview | q101, q102 ... | One question an interviewer asks an AI engineer, taught as a series (`docs-notes/interview-teaching-flow.md`, ladder `roadmap/interview-series-draft.json`). Live, 2 a day. |
| generic | e101, e102 ... | RETIRED for now (owner, 2026-10-19). Latest AI tools and features, agent skills, remarkable repos. |

The work day builds three reels: one series day and two interview reels. A retired track keeps its ids and its finished reels; nothing is planned or built for it until the owner brings it back.

Every reel is stand-alone: reels are shown at random, so each one makes its own topic clear.

- `state/learnings.md`: rules proven on our own numbers. They beat generic advice and the skills; read it before you start. Agents never edit it by hand. ## Guardrails
These are the hard limits (true facts, safety, no posting, the owner's final say). Inside them, use your judgment: where a rule or a prompt step makes the reel worse, do it better and say so.
1. **Facts.** Every factual claim (numbers, dates, versions, prices, "latest", what a product does) needs a URL you fetched in this session with WebFetch. Put the URLs in the queue entry and the spec. A claim you cannot verify is dropped or labelled as an example. A README claim like "98 % faster" is the author's claim: say so.
2. **Stand-alone.** One idea per reel. Define every term inside the reel. Never write "Part N", "next video" or a series tease.
3. **Voice and Hinglish.** The voice is the owner's own (ElevenLabs "Shivam Trainer", owner decision 2026-10-17; model Flash v2.5 at tempo 0.9 for new reels, owner decision 2026-10-19) by default, and Swara (Edge TTS) when the owner's voice is not available or its monthly characters are used up; in the studio the office tool `make_voice` picks it. The narration source has Hindi words in Devanagari (both voices read them) and English words in Roman letters. On-screen captions are Roman Hinglish (`make_reel_audio.py` romanizes them; the voice is unchanged). Every text we post (Instagram and Facebook caption, YouTube title and description) is Roman Hinglish with no Devanagari. Add a `{Devanagari}` spelling only when the voice says one word wrongly, one word per brace (`ChatGPT{चैटजीपीटी}`); never a multi-word brace.
4. **Hook.** The first spoken line (at most 10 words; for `interviewer_asked` the question may run to 16) gives the payoff or the question within two seconds. Score its Roman form with the hookscore script; a DEALBREAKER means rewrite it. Every item records its `hook_type` (`.claude/skills/hook-writer`): course `direct_question`; interview a rotation of `relatable_stake`, `interviewer_asked` (ONE clear, complete, self-contained question) and `company_specific` (only with a fetched page; record `company` and `company_source`); generic `surprising_fact` and `misconception_question` side by side (a belief the viewer holds, answered by the source; owner 2026-10-17). No hook type that works is discarded: types are tested side by side and retired only on our own numbers. The interview hooks stay as they are (owner, 2026-10-19).
5. **Length** 20 to 180 seconds. A series day (a### once the series is wired) runs as long as the teaching needs (owner, 2026-10-19: 2:30 to 3:00 is fine; value first, crystal clear, a human teacher's flow; never padded, never rushed); Instagram's reel limit is 3 minutes and Facebook Reels stop at 90 seconds, so a longer day goes to Instagram only. Interview reels run 90 to 120 seconds, about 230 to 300 spoken words (owner 2026-10-19: the interview reels get the teaching quality of the series days; earlier "reel 90s hogi chalega", 2026-10-18). Clarity beats brevity. Voice pace 1.3.
6. **Covers are clean English.** Instagram cover (1080x1920): one headline of at most 8 English words, one small gold pill with the category only (WHAT'S NEW IN AI, AI ENGINEER ROADMAP, AI ENGINEER INTERVIEW), the handle, and the LearnAI robot Bit as the only illustration (`scripts/render_cover.py`). No item count, no "Part N", no Hindi, no emoji. YouTube thumbnail (1280x720): at most 3 English words, not a copy of the title.
7. **Packaging.** Captions pass the ig-caption linter (no FAIL). The YouTube title follows yt-package, the description follows yt-seo.
8. **The owner approves.** Finished reels wait for the owner in the office (a plan or a script only when the owner asks to see it; then it goes with `request_approval` before any audio or visuals are made). The automatic gate (`scripts/ci_verify_reel.py`, with the independent visual review and fact check) only holds back a reel that is broken (a file missing, not 1080x1920 h264, no audio, length outside 20 to 180 seconds, frozen frames). Its other checks (mute test, layout, hook score, term count, caption lint, loudness, bookkeeping, a fact finding) are notes shown on the reel: the owner reviews every reel and decides (owner 2026-10-18: no failing on things that do not matter). Aim to pass them anyway, they are advice.
9. **No posting.** Agents never post, reply, like, follow or DM. The owner posts from the Calendar tab.
10. **Engagement.** Comment replies are drafts for the owner. Reels may ask viewers to comment a keyword ("follow karo, phir comment karo PROMPT, guide DM mein"); the owner's own automation answers. Never copy other people's comment text or usernames into the repo.
11. **Safety.** No medical, financial or legal advice. No real people's likeness or voice. Logos, real screenshots and references are welcome when they make the reel concrete (owner decision 2026-10-18): the logo of the company or product the reel names, a screenshot of the real page, job post, release note, repo or app it talks about. Credit the source on screen (a small "Source: <site>" label), use a logo to identify, never to suggest that company endorses LearnAI, and blur private people's names, emails and faces in screenshots. In the studio, fetch them with the office tools `fetch_image` and `capture_page`. Music only from the bundled bed. Disclose AI content where the platform supports it. Avoid repos or hacks that break a provider's terms, anomalous repos (impossible star counts), and voice cloning without a consent line.
12. **Memory.** Before you plan, read ai-course's `state/covered.json` (`mcp__office__read_ai_course`) and this folder's `state/covered.json`. When you plan something, add it to this folder's `state/covered.json`. Nothing from the last 60 days comes back as the same item.
13. **Scripts in the owner's voice.** When the owner gives wording, keep it: shorten by deleting whole lines, never rewrite English-mixed lines into stiff translated Hindi. Keep the beats the owner asked for (for interview: the seven beats of `docs-notes/interview-teaching-flow.md`). End with the owner's CTA when the reel offers a guide.
14. **Generic track (owner, 2026-10-17; retired for now, 2026-10-19)**: latest tools and features, agent skills, remarkable GitHub repos, "how to use AI to be 10x". Never promise a literal 10x: the honest framing comes from primary sources (for example METR's 2025 study found experienced developers 19 % slower while they felt faster). Verify at the primary source (release notes, the repo itself).
15. **Visuals are diagrams, not words.** Every beat is a picture of the idea, synced to the voice. On-screen text is a label of one to three words, never the sentence being spoken. The mute test decides. Process: `visual_plan.md` before the code, then build, `scripts/lint_visuals.py`, snapshots, render, `scripts/visual_review.py`. Clean style (`.claude/skills/clean-reel`): no glow, flicker, glitch, flash or sparkle; calm colours; `"audio": "v2"`.
16. **Benchmarks** teach format and strategy only. Never copy another channel's scripts, wording, visuals, audio, mascot or topic list.

## Working locally (same commands on the runner)
- Scaffold: `python scripts/new_reel.py <id>_<slug> "<title>" "<CATEGORY>"`.
- Audio: `python scripts/make_reel_audio.py reels/<folder>`. Render: `python scripts/render_job.py start reels/<folder>`, then `wait`.
- HyperFrames is pinned: `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.149 <check|snapshot|render>`.
- Lints: `python scripts/validate_queue.py`, `python scripts/lint_script.py`, `python scripts/lint_visuals.py reels/<folder>`. Gate: `python scripts/ci_verify_reel.py <id>`.
